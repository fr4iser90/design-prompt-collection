Build a playable Canvas 2D physics toy titled 'Harmonic Chorus'. The player controls a single pendulum suspended from a fixed pivot at the top-center of the canvas. If the pendulum hits the glass without resonance, it bounces off (fail state for that attempt, time penalty -5s). If the pendulum swings too long without hitting the target or shattering it, a timer expires, ending the run. Score = Shattered Glass Count × Frequency Accuracy (0-100%). Input: Drag vertical slider for length, Space to release/reset swing, Click to reset level. HUD displays current length, estimated frequency, and timer.
