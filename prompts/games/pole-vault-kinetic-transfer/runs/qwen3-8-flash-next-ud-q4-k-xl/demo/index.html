The Technique

01
Run

The athlete sprints automatically. Build horizontal speed — that momentum is your raw material.

02
Plant

Press and hold to jam the pole into the box. It bends, storing elastic energy. Watch the tension meter climb.

03
Release

Let go inside the gold window. Peak tension converts to vertical velocity — the launch vector follows the bend.

04
Rotate

Use the arrow keys to spin the body. Get the hips over the bar before the legs to keep it up.

05
Clear

Land on the mat without touching the bar. Each success raises the height by 0.5 m. Snap or knock it down and the run ends.
