# Concept Kong Scroll — Extended

## Concept
Process-as-motion for Northmark Studio: briefing→result as one continuous graphic system. Sharp, slightly irreverent — not SaaS.

## Scene sheet
| Progress | Phase | Visual |
|----------|-------|--------|
| 0.00–0.12 | Briefing | Point + grid |
| 0.12–0.28 | Idee | Split forms |
| 0.28–0.45 | Konzept | Composed structure |
| 0.45–0.62 | Design | Type/lines/planes |
| 0.62–0.80 | System | Multiplied apps |
| 0.80–1.00 | Ergebnis | Brand + Northmark Studio |

Autoplay: progress 0→0.35 in ~2s; scroll continues; Space resets.

## Poster frame
At ~1.5s (≈0.3–0.4): **Concept is King** amid composed forms; chalk on ink; kong accent as one structural line. 1200×630 readable.
