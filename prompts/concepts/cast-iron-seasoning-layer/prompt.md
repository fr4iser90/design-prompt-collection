# Cast Iron Seasoning Layer

Create a conceptual visualization of the cast iron seasoning process. 

**Visuals:**
- Central element: A macro cross-section of cast iron surface.
- Layers: Show the raw iron (dark grey, porous), then layers of polymerized oil (translucent amber/brown) accumulating.
- Interaction: A 'Heat' slider that causes the oil layers to cure and darken, turning from sticky yellow to hard black polymer.
- Background: Matte black with subtle steel blue ambient light from above (kitchen hood).
- Typography: Clean, sans-serif labels for 'Iron', 'Oil', 'Polymer'. Small monospace data for temperature (375°F - 450°F).

**Motion:**
- On load: Raw iron fades in.
- On hover: Show microscopic pores.
- On slider drag: Oil layers apply and cure in real-time, with a slight glow effect during polymerization.
