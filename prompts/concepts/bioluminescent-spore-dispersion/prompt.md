# Bioluminescent Spore Dispersion

Create a WebGL canvas experience simulating bioluminescent spores in a dark forest at night.

**Visual Rules:**
- Background: Deep void black (#050a0f) with subtle vertical gradients suggesting deep foliage shadows.
- Spores: Soft, glowing particles (cyan/green spectrum #00f5d4 to #7bffcd). Size variance: 2px to 8px.
- Motion: Slow, upward drift with gentle lateral sway (noise-based).
- Interaction: Mouse cursor acts as a 'breath' force. Spores near the cursor are gently pushed away and brighten briefly before fading back.
- No harsh edges. Use additive blending for overlapping glows.
- Typography: None. Pure visual atmosphere.
