# Oxidized Coin Valuation

Create a financial/data card featuring a **tarnished coin**.

**Visual Metaphor**:
- The card displays a circular, metallic object covered in dark, organic 'patina' (oxidation).
- **Interaction**: The user 'cleans' the coin by moving their cursor over it. This acts like a mask or eraser, revealing the bright, polished metal underneath.
- **Reveal**: As more of the coin is cleaned, a **numerical value** (e.g., `$1,204.50`) fades in on the coin's surface. The value is only fully visible when the coin is 'clean'.

**Style**:
- **Patina**: Dark brown/green textures, irregular edges, semi-transparent overlay.
- **Metal**: High-contrast gold or silver with specular highlights.
- **Card**: Minimal, dark background. No borders. The coin is the hero.

**Motion**:
- The cleaning effect should have a 'trail' (smudge effect) rather than a hard cut.
- Ambient light reflections on the metal move as the cursor moves.

**Constraints**:
- No standard 'hover to reveal' opacity transitions.
- Use masking or canvas for the cleaning effect.
- The 'value' should feel embossed into the metal.
