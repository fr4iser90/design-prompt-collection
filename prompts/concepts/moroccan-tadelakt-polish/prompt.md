# Moroccan Tadelakt Polish

**Intent**: Simulate the process of polishing Tadelakt (Moroccan lime plaster). Start with a rough, matte, sandy surface. Users drag a 'polishing stone' across it to create smooth, glossy, water-resistant patches.

**Visual Rules**:
1.  **Base Surface**: Rough, sandy texture in warm beige/terracotta tones (`#d2b48c`). Visible grain.
2.  **Polished Surface**: Smooth, high-gloss, slightly reflective. Color deepens slightly (`#a0522d` or `#8fbc8f` depending on variant). Specular highlights are sharp and bright.
3.  **Tool**: A circular, smooth 'river stone' cursor. As it moves, it transforms the underlying texture from rough to smooth.
4.  **Reflection**: The polished area should reflect a subtle, moving light source (like a window or sun), mimicking the soapstone finish of real Tadelakt.
5.  **Water Droplets**: Optional overlay. Clicking adds a water droplet that beads up on polished areas (high contact angle) but spreads on rough areas.

**Deliverable**: HTML/CSS/JS. Use WebGL or Canvas for texture mixing. Focus on the tactile feel of the transformation.
