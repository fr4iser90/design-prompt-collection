# Tidal Glass Horizon

Design a minimal landing hero featuring a dynamic horizon line that rises and falls like a tide.

**Visual Rules:**
1. **The Lens:** A curved, invisible glass lens effect is centered on the horizon. Text passing 'through' the water line is slightly magnified and chromatically aberrated (RGB split) at the edges.
2. **Palette:** Saline and airy. Background is off-white (`#f0f4f8`). The 'water' area is a translucent blue-gray (`#a3c4dc`). Horizon line is thin and crisp (`#2c3e50`).
3. **Motion:** The horizon moves slowly (breathing animation). Text is static until the horizon passes over it, causing a subtle 'ripple' distortion.
4. **Content:** Large, elegant serif typeface. Minimal navigation. Focus on whitespace and negative space.

**Deliverable:** A high-fidelity HTML/CSS mockup with JS-driven horizon animation.
