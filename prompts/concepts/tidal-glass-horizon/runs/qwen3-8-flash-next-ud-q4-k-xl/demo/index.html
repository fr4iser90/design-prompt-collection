Tidal Glass Horizon

Look at the water, not into it — where stillness is a surface and light is the only thing that moves.

Enter the study

The tide is the only interface.

Everything here behaves like a calm plane of glass water. Words rest on the surface; the horizon breathes beneath them, bending their edges just enough to remind you the light is being refracted. No splashes. No bubbles. Only the slow arithmetic of salt and sky.

01 / Refraction

The Lens

An invisible curved lens sits centred on the horizon. As a word crosses the waterline it magnifies slightly and splits into a whisper of chromatic fringe.

02 / Stillness

Glass Water

The surface never breaks. Motion is limited to a single vertical breath — a tide rising and falling across a ten-second loop, no more.

03 / Air

Saline Space

Off-white sky, desaturated blue, slate ink. Negative space is the material; the palette is the weather.
