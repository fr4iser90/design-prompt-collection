# Gasket Compression Diagram — Extended Brief

## Concept
Sealing is about pressure. This concept visualizes the invisible force of compression by showing the visible deformation of a gasket. It’s a study of softness resisting hardness. The mood is calm, methodical, and secure.

## Palette
- **Metal:** `#485460` (Dark Slate Grey) with linear gradients for metallic sheen.
- **Rubber:** `#1e272e` (Almost black, matte).
- **Contact Highlight:** `#a4b0be` (Soft light grey).
- **Background:** `#f5f6fa` (Very light cool grey).

## Type Pairing
- **Labels:** IBM Plex Sans (Industrial, readable)
- **Numbers:** IBM Plex Mono

## Layout
- **Desktop:** Centered diagram. Labels appear to the left and right, connected by thin leader lines.
- **Mobile:** Diagram on top. Labels below in a simple list.

## Motion Brief
1. **Entrance:** Flanges slide in from top and bottom, leaving a gap for the gasket.
2. **Ambient:** Idle state: The gasket is slightly compressed (pre-loaded).
3. **Interaction:** 
   - **Scroll/Slide:** User drags a slider to apply "Torque." The top flange moves down.
   - **Deformation:** The gasket height decreases. Its width increases (bulge). 
   - **Seal Check:** At 100% compression, the contact points glow softly, and a "Seal Intact" checkmark fades in.
   - **Release:** If torque is reduced, the rubber springs back (hysteresis).

## Constraints
- The rubber deformation must be physically plausible (volume preservation).
- No bouncy, cartoonish springs. The motion should feel heavy and damped.
- Keep the background light and airy to contrast with the dark rubber.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The bulging effect is visible and satisfying.
- The transition between "loose" and "sealed" is clear.
- The overall feel is "engineering calm."
