# Gasket Compression Diagram

**Concept:** A cross-section view of two metal flanges squeezing a rubber gasket. The focus is on the material deformation—how the rubber bulges slightly when compressed.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Layers:** Top Flange (Metal Grey), Gasket (Dark Charcoal Rubber), Bottom Flange (Metal Grey).
- **Motion:** The flanges move down slowly. The gasket compresses, losing height but gaining width (bulging sideways).
- **Feedback:** When the seal is "tight," a subtle, soft glow or highlight appears at the contact points.
- **Style:** Technical diagram, but rendered with soft, realistic materials. Not wireframe.

**Deliverable:** An SVG animation or Canvas simulation showing the compression cycle.
