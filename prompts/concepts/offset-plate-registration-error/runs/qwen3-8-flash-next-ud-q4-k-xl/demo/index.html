On Registration

In offset lithography, four separate plates must print in perfect register. A drift of a few microns blurs the edge into a vibrating halo — the misregistration that once ruined a run. Here the ghost is the opening state; alignment is the payoff.

On Subtractive Mixing

No colour is faked. Cyan, Magenta and Yellow are laid down as multiply layers, so overlap produces genuine secondaries — C+M=Blue, M+Y=Red, C+Y=Green — exactly as the ink would on the sheet.
