# Night Blooming Time-Lapse — Extended

**Concept:**
The drama of the night is in its slow reveal. This concept captures the anticipation of a flower opening only in the dark, a secret visible only to moths and moonlight. The mood is romantic, mysterious, and serene. Avoid any 'daytime' brightness or harsh contrast.

**Palette:**
- **Sky:** `#030508` (Deep indigo-black)
- **Petal Shadow:** `#2e1f2b` (Dark plum/muted purple for depth in folds)
- **Petal Highlight:** `#a8dadc` (Pale, cool white/blue, catching moonlight)
- **Stamen:** `#e0d5c1` (Dull, pale gold, barely visible)

**Type Pairing:**
- Minimal. If text is present, use a delicate serif (e.g., Cormorant Garamond) in `#a8dadc`, fading in slowly as the flower opens.

**Layout:**
- Centered subject.
- Vertical format preferred for mobile, or wide crop for desktop focusing on the bloom.
- No UI elements. Immersive.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Fade from black to the closed bud.
- **Ambient:** Slight sway of the stem (very subtle, wind-like).
- **Main Action:** 
  - 0-50%: Outer petals loosen and tilt outward.
  - 50-80%: Inner petals unfurl, revealing the center.
  - 80-100%: Full bloom, hold for 2 seconds, then reverse or fade.
- **Easing:** `ease-in-out` for all movements. No linear motion.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] No cottagecore (no bunnies, no fences, no pastel pinks).
- [ ] No daytime greens; focus on dark, cool tones.
- [ ] Motion must be smooth, not stop-motion jerky.
- [ ] Lighting must be consistent (moonlight, not sunlight).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Evokes a sense of calm and wonder.
- The bloom feels organic and natural.
- Colors are muted and sophisticated.
