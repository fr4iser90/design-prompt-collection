# Night Blooming Time-Lapse

Create a looping animation of a night-blooming flower opening in slow motion against a dark, starry night sky.

**Visual Rules:**
- Subject: A single flower bud (e.g., Cereus or Moonflower) with layered petals.
- Motion: Extremely slow, elegant unfurling. Petals rotate and separate gently. No jitter.
- Lighting: Cold, ambient moonlight from above. Soft shadows within the petal layers.
- Background: Deep night sky (#030508) with faint, static stars or a subtle moon glow.
- Style: Photorealistic or high-end 3D render. Soft depth of field (blur on background).
- Loop: Seamless transition from closed bud to full bloom and back, or a fade-to-black restart.
