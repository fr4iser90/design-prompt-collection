# Salt Flat Horizon Alignment

Create a minimalist conceptual layout evoking the vastness of a salt flat at noon.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** A seamless, bone-white gradient representing the salt crust. No horizon line is visible initially; the ground and sky are indistinguishable in brightness.
- **Horizon Reveal:** On scroll or hover, a faint, dark line emerges at the vertical center, establishing the horizon. Shadows cast from elements extend infinitely toward the viewer, lengthening as they approach the bottom of the screen.
- **Typography:** Ultra-light sans-serif text floats slightly above the surface. The text is centered horizontally. As elements move closer to the foreground, they scale up slightly and their shadows deepen and stretch.
- **Atmosphere:** Subtle heat haze distortion at the very bottom edge. Dust motes drift slowly.

**Deliverable:** A single HTML/CSS/JS component demonstrating the horizon reveal and shadow perspective.
