# Porcelain Insulator Hysteresis

**Concept:** Visualize the electrical hysteresis loop of a porcelain insulator not as a jagged graph, but as a smooth, continuous curve carved into white ceramic.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** Deep, matte charcoal (`#2d3436`) with a subtle, radial vignette.
- **Subject:** A single, large, white porcelain insulator shape (ribbed profile) centered. 
- **Motion:** A glowing, thin cyan line traces the hysteresis loop (B-H curve) on the surface of the porcelain. The line moves slowly, smoothly, with no flicker.
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif (e.g., Söhne or Helvetica Neue) in light grey. Labels for "Saturation" and "Coercivity" appear only when the line reaches those points.
- **Materiality:** The porcelain should have a slight subsurface scattering effect, looking cold and smooth.

**Deliverable:** An interactive SVG/Canvas component where hovering slows the trace; clicking resets the cycle.
