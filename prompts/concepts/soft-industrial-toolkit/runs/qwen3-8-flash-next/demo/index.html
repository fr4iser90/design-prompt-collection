07 — Motion

Three primitives. Nothing else.

M-01

Soft settle

On load: opacity 0→1 with 6px rise. The page lands like an object set down.

M-02

Control press

Active state travels exactly 1px and flattens its shadow. Press and release.

M-03

Copper grow

On focus, the accent line grows from zero. The only place copper moves.

All motion honors prefers-reduced-motion — the system renders fully static.

08 — Guidelines

Photography & motion, in two columns.

✓Photography — Do
  • Shoot objects on porcelain and aluminum grounds with soft, directional light.
  • Let the brand name dominate the frame; the product supports it.
  • Keep surfaces matte — brushed metal grain, not mirror reflections.
  • Use copper as a single signal point, never a color wash.
✕Photography — Don't
  • No purple or neon SaaS glow, no gradient backdrops.
  • No rustic farmhouse props — linen, twine, wood-grain clichés.
  • No skeuomorphic knobs, dials, or cartoon textures.
  • No glossy "product render" sheen that reads as software.
✓Motion — Do
  • Settle content in with a 6px, 0.7s opacity rise.
  • Give controls a 1px press travel with a flattening shadow.
  • Grow the copper accent line on focus and active states.
  • Keep timing calm — ease-out, no bounce.
✕Motion — Don't
  • No parallax, tilt, or scroll-jacking gimmicks.
  • No springy overshoot or playful bounce easing.
  • No animated blobs or morphing shapes.
  • Never ignore prefers-reduced-motion.
