# Photocopy Contrast Degradation

Design a UI that mimics the degradation of a document through repeated photocopying. The aesthetic is high-contrast, grainy, and imperfect.

**Visual Rules:**
1. **Base State:** Clean, sharp black text and lines on white. High contrast.
2. **Degradation:** As the user scrolls or as elements move further from the viewport center, they gain 'noise' (static), lose sharpness (blur), and the contrast increases (whites get whiter, blacks get blacker, losing mid-tones).
3. **Artifacts:** Add subtle 'streaks' and 'dust spots' that appear randomly on degraded elements. Edges of text become jagged and broken.
4. **Interaction:** Clicking an element 'resets' it to the clean state, but it will degrade again over time or upon scrolling away.

**Deliverable:** A content-rich page (e.g., a manifesto or zine cover) where the typography and images progressively distort into photocopy artifacts based on scroll position.
