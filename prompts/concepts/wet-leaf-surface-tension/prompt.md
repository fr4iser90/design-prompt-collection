# Wet Leaf Surface Tension

Design a high-fidelity interactive macro scene focusing on water droplets on a dark, waxy leaf surface at night.

**Visual Rules:**
- Surface: Deep, dark green-black leaf texture with high gloss/waxiness.
- Droplets: Physically simulated water beads. High surface tension (perfect spheres or slightly flattened ovals).
- Light Source: Cool, directional moonlight (top-left). Creates sharp, small specular highlights on droplets.
- Interaction: Hovering over the leaf surface causes droplets to coalesce (merge) with neighbors or roll off the edge if near the border.
- Reflection: Droplets act as fisheye lenses, distorting the dark background color slightly.
- No cartoonish water. Use realistic refraction indices.
