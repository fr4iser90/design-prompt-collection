# Kintsugi Gold Seam Heal

**Intent**: Visualize the Japanese art of kintsugi (golden joinery) as a digital interface element. Show a broken ceramic vessel being repaired in real-time, emphasizing the beauty of the cracks rather than hiding them.

**Visual Rules**:
1.  **Background**: Deep charcoal or matte black to make the gold pop. No clutter.
2.  **Subject**: A 3D-rendered ceramic bowl (white or earthy tone) shattered into 3-5 major pieces.
3.  **The Repair**: Gold leaf lines trace the fractures. The lines should look raised and textured, not flat.
4.  **Interaction**: Hovering over a shard pulls it slightly toward its neighbor. Clicking initiates the 'healing' animation where gold lacquer flows between the gaps, sealing them.
5.  **Typography**: Minimalist sans-serif (e.g., 'Zen' or 'Inter' alternative like 'Lato' but cleaner) in white or gold. Low contrast until active.
6.  **Motion**: Slow, deliberate. The flow of gold should mimic viscous liquid, not instant snap.

**Deliverable**: Single HTML/CSS/JS file. Use SVG for the gold seams for crisp rendering. Ensure mobile touch support for the 'pull' interaction.
