# Desert Varnish Patina Map

Design a conceptual interface where data layers are revealed through the simulation of 'Desert Varnish' (manganese/iron oxide coating on rocks).

**Visual Rules:**
- **Substrate:** A rough, porous sandstone texture in light clay tones (`#D2B48C`).
- **Varnish Layer:** A semi-transparent, dark brown/black overlay (`#5C4033`) that appears over time. It is not uniform; it pools in crevices and thins on ridges.
- **Data Reveal:** Users interact with a 'time slider' or scroll progress. As time advances, the dark varnish stains specific areas, revealing text or icons underneath the patina. The reveal looks like natural staining, not a mask wipe.
- **Texture:** High-contrast grain. The varnish must have an organic, speckled quality.

**Deliverable:** A React component or SVG animation where scrolling 'stains' the rock surface to reveal content.
