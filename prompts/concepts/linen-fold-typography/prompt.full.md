# Linen Fold Typography — Extended

**Concept:**
A departure from rigid, digital type. This concept treats typography as a textile. Letters are not drawn; they are *folded*. This creates a sense of softness, weight, and physical presence. The interface feels like handling fine cloth.

**Palette:**
- **Background:** `#f5f5dc` (Linen white, warm, off-white).
- **Fabric Base:** `#d2b48c` (Natural tan, linen weave color).
- **Shadow/Depth:** `#8b7355` (Warm brown, used for creases and drop shadows).

**Typography:**
- **Font:** A rounded, organic sans-serif or serif with soft terminals (e.g., Avenir or a custom serif). No sharp angles.
- **Treatment:** Text is applied to a 3D-like cloth surface with normal maps for fabric texture.

**Layout:**
- **Hero:** Large, headline text that looks like it’s been pressed into linen.
- **Body:** Smaller text, appearing as if printed on a finer, single-ply cloth.
- **Whitespace:** Generous, allowing the "fabric" to breathe and cast shadows.

**Motion Brief:**
1.  **Entrance:** Text "unfolds" from a flat state, with creases animating into place.
2.  **Ambient:** Subtle, slow breathing motion (scale 1.0 to 1.01) to suggest softness.
3.  **Interaction:** 
    -   **Hover:** Letter lifts slightly, shadow expands and softens. Fabric texture becomes more visible.
    -   **Click:** Word "drapes" downward with a spring physics simulation.

**Constraints:**
-   No hard edges or sharp shadows.
-   Fabric texture must be visible at high zoom.
-   Motion must be soft and fluid, no abrupt snaps.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
-   Text feels soft and tactile, not digital.
-   Shadows contribute to the perception of depth and weight.
-   No standard UI elements (buttons, inputs).
