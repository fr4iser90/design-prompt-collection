# Linen Fold Typography

Create a typographic system where letters are rendered as folded linen fabric.

**Core Metaphor:**
- Each letter is a piece of linen cloth.
- The "weight" of the type is determined by the thickness of the fabric and the number of folds.
- Bold letters have deeper creases and more pronounced shadows.
- Light letters are single-ply, soft, and draped.

**Interaction:**
- Hovering over a letter "unfolds" it slightly, revealing more texture and a softer shadow.
- Clicking a word causes it to "drape" over the next line, like fabric falling.

**Visual Style:**
- **Background:** Off-white, smooth paper texture (`#f5f5dc`).
- **Fabric:** Natural linen color (`#d2b48c`) with visible weave texture.
- **Shadows:** Soft, diffused, warm-toned (`#8b7355` at low opacity).
- **No outlines or borders.** The form is defined by the fabric edges and shadows.
