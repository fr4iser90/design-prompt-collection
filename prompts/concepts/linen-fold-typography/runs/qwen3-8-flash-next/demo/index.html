Linen Fold

Letters are not drawn here. They are pressed, creased and draped — each glyph a length of linen whose weight sets the depth of its shadow.

Weights read as ply. Lighter faces are single-ply and drape; heavier faces carry deeper creases.

Hover a letter to unfold · tap a word to drape

01 — Weight as Ply

The heavier the cloth, the deeper the crease.

Weight is not a stroke width. It is fabric thickness — how many plies are folded together, and how far the shadow falls off the fold beneath.

02 — The Weave

Zoom in and the threads appear.

Every surface carries a cross-hatch of warp and weft. The texture is generated, not photographed — so it stays crisp at any magnification, catching light along the fold.

03 — The Cloth Moves

Four gestures, all soft.

Nothing snaps. Fabric settles, lifts and falls with weight — motion borrowed from how real cloth behaves when you handle it.
