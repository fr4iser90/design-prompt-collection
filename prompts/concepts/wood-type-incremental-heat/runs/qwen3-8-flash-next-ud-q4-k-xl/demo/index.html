03 Cutting the Block

  1. Season the stock

    Maple end-grain is kiln-dried and planed true so the surface holds ink evenly and the grain runs clean across the face.

  2. Pantograph the letter

    A tracer copies a pattern onto the block; a cutter follows, sinking the relief a few thousandths deep.

  3. Set the bed

    Blocks are locked into the chase with furniture and quoins, spaced by the em, and leveled to bear even pressure.

  4. Ink & pull

    A brayer lays a thin film of ink. The press closes, the fibers compress, and the image prints — warm, dense, alive.
