Our Method

Colour that submits to the light.

Every bolt begins saturated and ends surrendered. We leave our weaves on the drying terraces through the long summer, letting the sun pull the pigment down until the hue turns chalky and soft — the way a cliff face fades over centuries.

The result is a palette that never shouts. It absorbs the harsh desert light and gives back something muted, tactile, and unhurried. These are fabrics meant to be lived in, not looked at.

  • 01
    Slow FadeNatural sun-bleaching over 40+ days — no chemical agents, no shortcuts.
  • 02
    Low SaturationEvery tone is pulled below 30% chroma for a unified, serene hand.
  • 03
    Weight & HandHeavier cloths carry more shadow; lighter ones catch more light.
