# Sun-Bleached Textile Swatches

Create a product selector for 'Arid Weave' brand, showcasing 5 fabric swatches.

**Visual Rules:**
- Background: Off-white linen texture (#F0EBE1).
- Swatches: Rectangular blocks (150x200px) with realistic fabric textures (linen, silk, wool) that appear faded/sun-bleached.
- Shadows: Long, sharp, 60-degree angle shadows (box-shadow: 10px 10px 0px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)) to simulate harsh desert sun.
- Typography: 'Didot' for names, 'Lato' for descriptions. All caps for names.

**Interaction:**
- Hover: Swatch lifts (translateY(-5px)) and shadow expands. A 'light sweep' gradient moves across the fabric texture (background-position animation).
- Click: Swatch expands to full width, revealing detailed specs (GSM, Origin, Care).

**Constraint:**
- Colors must be desaturated. No vibrant colors. Use only earth tones and neutrals. The aesthetic is 'worn by the sun'.
