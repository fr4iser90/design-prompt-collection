# Nocturnal Garden Moodboard — Extended

## Concept
A garden that only blooms after eyes adjust. Light is biological, not LED strip. Type feels engraved on leaf dark.

## Art direction
- Palette: soil night `#070b0a`, leaf dark `#143028`, dew `#cfe7d6`, lumen `#9dffb0`, pollen `#e6d67a` (rare)
- Type: a lyrical serif (e.g. Cormorant / Reckless) + tiny mono captions
- Photo: macro wet leaves, moth-adjacent blur, practical lumen points
- Texture: velvet noise; soft chromatic aberration banned

## Moodboard slots
1. Color chips on black field
2. Brand wordmark specimen
3. Serif / mono pairing proof
4. Three photo crops (leaf / path / bloom)
5. Texture swatch
6. Motion notes as storyboard frames
7. Forbidden board (purple glow, cottage pastel, stock “fairy lights”)

## Motion principles (future)
1. Dew points shimmer at 0.5 opacity delta
2. Slow vertical drift on background botanicals
3. Type ink-in on enter
