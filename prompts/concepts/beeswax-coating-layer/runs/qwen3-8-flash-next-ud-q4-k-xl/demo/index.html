Care is a layer you apply, not a thing you own. Beeswax holds the grain against time — a thin, breathing skin of warmth.

On the finish

Raw material is honest but vulnerable. Cold to the touch, it drinks moisture and dulls under use. A coat of beeswax changes the conversation: it seals without suffocating, deepens without hiding.

The layer is never opaque. It is a gradient of translucency — thin at the edges where light passes, thick at the heart where it pools and glows. Readability survives the finish; the surface simply becomes kinder.
