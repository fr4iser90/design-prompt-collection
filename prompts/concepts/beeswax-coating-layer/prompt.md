# Beeswax Coating Layer

Create a conceptual interface where a dark, matte black substrate (representing raw material) is gradually coated with a semi-translucent, viscous beeswax layer.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Substrate:** Deep charcoal/black with subtle wood grain or paper texture.
- **Wax Layer:** Amber-to-gold gradient, semi-transparent, with specular highlights that shift based on 'thickness'.
- **Interaction:** As the user scrolls or hovers, the wax layer 'melts' into the substrate, changing opacity and saturation.
- **Type:** Serif, elegant, partially obscured by the wax layer until fully melted.

**Deliverable:** A single interactive section showing the transition from raw dark material to rich, waxed warmth.
