Style Bible

1

Bevel honesty. Light source is top-left. Every metal plate carries a highlight along its top/left edge and a shadow along its bottom/right edge — never flat.

2

Silhouette readability. Limbs separate from the torso at 64px. Internal rivets and panels never break the outer outline.

3

Articulation. Visible hinges, rivets and pistons. Joints move on mechanical logic — knees bend forward, gears mesh, pistons compress.

4

Materials. Brushed brass, oxidized copper, polished steel and dark iron. Walk cycles show weight shift; strikes show anticipation & follow-through.
