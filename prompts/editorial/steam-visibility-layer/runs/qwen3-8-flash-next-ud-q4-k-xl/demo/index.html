CH. 01

The Science of Chill

Refrigeration / Physics

Condensation is not a flaw in the glass — it is evidence of the pour.

When the surface temperature of a vessel falls below the dew point of the surrounding air, water vapour loses its energy and collapses back into liquid. What we read as a cold drink is, in truth, a small weather system forming on the outside of the glass.

The heavier the humidity, the faster the film gathers. The colder the pour, the crisper the contrast between the dark cellar and the bright bead of moisture. A sommelier reads the glass the way a meteorologist reads a sky — the fog is the forecast.
