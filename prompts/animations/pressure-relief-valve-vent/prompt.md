# Pressure Relief Valve Vent

**Goal:** Visualize the release of dangerous pressure through a mechanical vent, emphasizing the contrast between hot vapor and cold air.

**Visuals:**
- **Subject:** A heavy, dull brass (#B5A642) pressure relief valve mounted on a dark iron pipe. 
- **Gauge:** A circular analog gauge attached to the valve. The needle is initially deep in the red zone.
- **Steam:** A high-pressure jet of white steam erupts from the valve's side vent. The steam is dense, turbulent, and backlit.
- **Action:**
  1. The valve cap lifts slightly (a small vertical movement).
  2. Steam bursts out in a sharp, conical jet.
  3. As the steam flows, the gauge needle sweeps downward from red to green.
  4. The steam jet tapers off and dissipates into thin wisps.
  5. The valve cap drops back into its seated position with a metallic 'thunk' (visualized by a slight recoil).

**Motion:**
- Steam particles: Use a particle system with upward drift and slight randomness. Opacity fades from 1.0 to 0.0.
- Gauge Needle: Smooth ease-out deceleration as it approaches the green zone.
- Valve Recoil: Quick shake on start and stop of the venting.

**Constraints:**
- The steam must look volumetric, not like simple smoke.
- The brass should look aged/used, not polished shiny.
- Background is pure black to make the white steam pop.
