# Linen Press Tension — Extended

**Concept:**
Hospitality is tactile. This animation focuses on the sensory experience of fine dining linen. It avoids the 'rustic farmhouse' cliché by keeping the linen stark white and the setting sterile and minimal. It’s about the quality of the weave and the weight of the ceramic.

**Palette:**
- **Linen:** #F5F5F0 (Warm Off-White) — Not pure white, to show texture.
- **Ceramic:** #FFFFFF (Pure White) — Slightly brighter than linen to distinguish layers.
- **Shadow:** #A9A9A9 (Dark Grey) — Soft, cool shadows.
- **Weave Detail:** #E0E0E0 (Light Grey) — For thread highlights.

**Typography:**
- Elegant serif (e.g., 'Playfair Display' or 'Didot') or a very refined humanist sans.
- Text should sit *on* the plate or be revealed as the plate presses down (masking).

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** Close-up crop of the press point. Text overlay on the plate or beside the folds.
- **Mobile:** Centered press animation. Text below.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Compression:** The core motion is the deformation of the fabric. Use a grid-based deformation or 3D cloth sim.
2. **Weave Interaction:** As the fabric stretches, the weave should slightly distort. Where it compresses, the weave densifies (darkens).
3. **Lighting Shift:** As folds form, the angle of light hitting the fabric changes, creating dynamic highlight/shadow interplay.
4. **Sound Design (Optional):** Muted, dry fabric rustle. No squeaks.

**Constraints:**
- No stains, spills, or cutlery.
- No warm, earthy colors. Strictly white/grey.
- Motion must be slow and deliberate, conveying weight and quality.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The fabric deformation looks realistic, not like a flat texture overlay.
- The weave texture is crisp and high-resolution.
- The overall mood is calm, clean, and luxurious.
