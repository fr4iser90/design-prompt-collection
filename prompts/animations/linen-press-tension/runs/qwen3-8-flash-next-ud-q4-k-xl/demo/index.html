The material

Not a texture overlay.
A deforming surface.

Every point of the sheet is a mass under tension. As the plate descends the grid compresses beneath the rim, threads densify and darken, and the surplus cloth is pushed outward into soft radial folds that carry their own weight and settle over time.

01 — Compression

It bunches, not flattens

Displacement is driven by a soft-body field. Under the rim the fabric gathers into wrinkles that propagate outward with momentum rather than pinning flat.

02 — Weave

The grain follows the fold

A crisp plain-weave is drawn per triangle. Where the cloth stretches the weave opens and brightens; where it compresses it tightens and darkens.

03 — Light

North light, soft shadows

Diffused daylight grazes the surface. Fold geometry re-angles the normals, so highlight and shadow shift continuously as the linen settles.
