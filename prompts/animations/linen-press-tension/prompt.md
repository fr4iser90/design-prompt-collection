# Linen Press Tension

Create a soft-body animation of a white ceramic plate slowly pressing into a sheet of textured linen fabric.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Material:** High-quality linen. Visible weave texture, slight irregularities in the thread thickness.
- **Object:** A smooth, matte or semi-gloss white ceramic plate. Clean lines, no chips.
- **Interaction:** The plate descends slowly. The linen compresses under the weight, forming soft, radial wrinkles that push outward.
- **Lighting:** Soft, diffused daylight (north light). Shadows should be soft but defined, emphasizing the depth of the folds.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Plate enters from top, slowly descending.
- **Physics:** Realistic soft-body simulation. The fabric should bunch up, not just squash. The wrinkles should have weight and momentum.
- **Ambient:** Subtle vibration or settling of the fabric after the plate touches down.
- **Interaction:** Cursor acts as a second press point or allows the user to 'lift' a corner of the linen slightly, showing elasticity.

**Deliverable:** A looping background animation or a hero visual.
