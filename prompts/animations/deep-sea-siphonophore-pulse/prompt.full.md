# Deep Sea Siphonophore Pulse — Extended

**Concept:**
The feeling of being crushed by depth, illuminated only by living light. This animation captures the quiet, hypnotic rhythm of deep-sea life. The subject is not just a jellyfish, but a colonial organism, suggesting complexity within simplicity. It embodies 'quiet luxury' through restraint and rare, high-value visual phenomena (bioluminescence).

**Palette:**
- **Abyssal Black:** #020408
- **Deep Ocean Blue:** #051224
- **Bioluminescent Cyan:** #00f0ff (used sparingly for emission)
- **Phosphorescent Green:** #00ffcc (secondary glow, lower opacity)
- **Marine Snow:** #ffffff (low opacity particles)

**Type & Layout:**
No text. The animation stands alone. If used in a landing page context, it serves as a full-bleed background with white, serif typography overlaid with high contrast.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Entrance:** The bell is contracted, dim. Over 2 seconds, it expands gently.
2. **Ambient:** The bell pulses. Each pulse releases a faint ring of light that dissipates. The tentacles trail behind, swaying with inertia.
3. **Interaction (if interactive):** Cursor proximity causes the organism to contract slightly (defensive reflex) or brighten (attractant).

**Constraints:**
- No neon 'glow' effects that look like digital errors. Light must feel refractive and organic.
- Avoid 'disco' lighting. Single light source (the organism).
- Frame rate must be smooth (60fps) to convey the weightlessness.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The pulse feels 'slow' and 'heavy', not bouncy.
- The transparency of the subject is convincing (you can see particles through the bell).
- The loop is seamless.
