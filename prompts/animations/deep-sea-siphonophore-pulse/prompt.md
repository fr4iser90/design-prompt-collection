# Deep Sea Siphonophore Pulse

Create an animation of a solitary, translucent jellyfish-like organism (siphonophore) pulsing in the abyss.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** Pitch black with a subtle, deep radial gradient (almost black to #050a14).
- **Subject:** A gelatinous, semi-transparent bell. Use noise filters to give it organic texture. No hard outlines.
- **Lighting:** Bioluminescent core. Light emanates from within, casting soft cyan (#00f0ff) caustics on the 'water' surface (simulated via subtle overlay).
- **Motion:** Slow, rhythmic expansion/contraction (6-8 second loop). The pulse should feel heavy, like moving through viscous fluid.
- **Particles:** Tiny, floating marine snow particles drifting upward slowly, catching the light.
- **Deliverable:** WebM or MP4 loop, 1080x1080, seamless.
