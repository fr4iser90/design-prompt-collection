# Caustic Light Refraction Tile

Create a seamless looping animation of sunlight refracting through water onto a tiled surface.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Perspective:** Top-down, looking through shallow, clear water onto a blue/teal tiled floor.
- **Effect:** Caustics. The lines of light should move organically, never repeating a predictable rhythm too quickly.
- **Palette:** Aquatic blues. Deep blues for shadows, bright cyan/white for light beams.
- **Motion:** The water surface ripples gently. The caustics shift and merge. Occasional 'dumbbell' shapes (converging light lines) appear.
- **Texture:** Subtle noise in the water for realism. Tiles are matte, not glossy.
- **Deliverable:** Seamless loop, 1080x1080, high resolution.
