# Caustic Light Refraction Tile — Extended

**Concept:**
The universal experience of looking into clear water. This animation leverages the 'aquatic-depth' lane to create a background that is both dynamic and meditative. It is not about the object in the water, but the *light* itself becoming a tangible, moving entity. It serves as a perfect 'quiet luxury' background for high-end brands.

**Palette:**
- **Deep Ocean:** #003b5c
- **Mid Water:** #006994
- **Shallow Light:** #00a8e8
- **Caustic Highlight:** #e0f7fa
- **Pure Light:** #ffffff

**Type & Layout:**
Designed to be a background layer. Text placed on top must have a drop shadow or solid background to maintain readability against the moving light.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Ambient:** Continuous, slow ripple. The caustics 'dance'—expanding and contracting.
2. **Intensity:** The brightness of the caustics should fluctuate subtly, as if clouds are passing over the sun.
3. **Loop:** Must be perfectly seamless. No 'jump' at the start/end frame.

**Constraints:**
- Avoid 'lava lamp' look. Caustics should be thin, web-like lines, not thick blobs.
- The water must look clear, not murky.
- No bubbles or foreign objects.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The caustic patterns feel naturally random yet coherent.
- The color palette is strictly aquatic blues/whites.
- The loop is invisible to the eye.
