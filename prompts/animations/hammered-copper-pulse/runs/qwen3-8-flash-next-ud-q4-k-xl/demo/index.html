01 THE MECHANICS

Heavy,
slow, and
deliberate.

The copper never snaps back. Every disturbance is met with solid-state friction — a high-damping field that bleeds energy fast, so each ripple collapses into the hammered grain within seconds. This is metal with mass, not a shader toy.

01

Idle Breathing

At rest the sheet is never truly still. A sub-pixel sine wave rolls across the vertices — an almost imperceptible respiration that keeps the specular glints drifting along the dimples.

The Lagging Dent

A pressure point trails the cursor with heavy inertia. It doesn't chase — it follows, sinking the surface behind your pointer like a thumb pressed into warm wax that cools too quickly to spring back.

Radial Pulse

Click, tap, or let the timed pulse fire every five seconds. A ring of displacement expands outward, carrying the hammered noise with it — stretching the grain at the crest, compressing it in the trough.

The hammered texture is generated procedurally — a domain-warped Voronoi cell field that reads as thousands of overlapping hammer strikes. It lives in the fragment shader as a normal perturbation, so it survives every deformation.

Because detail is a normal map rather than geometry, the sheet stays lightweight. Vertex displacement handles the macro ripples; the fragment stage handles the micro grain. Together they deform as one continuous surface.

Detail via normal map · Macro via vertex displacement · Zero high-poly cost

04 THE PIPELINE

How the sheet
remembers.

01Displacement Field

Accumulate the pressure

A ring buffer of active ripples — each with a center, a birth time, and a strength — is passed to the vertex shader. A persistent dent tracks the pointer with lagged inertia. Height is summed from every source into a single scalar field.

02Analytic Normals

Rebuild the surface

Instead of sampling a heightmap, the normal is derived analytically from the gradient of the displacement field. This keeps the hammered grain crisp even as the whole sheet bends, and costs nothing in geometry.

03Copper BRDF

Light the metal

A Blinn–Phong pass tints the specular toward gold (#F4C430) and the diffuse toward copper (#B87333). Fresnel lifts the grazing edges; a hard exponent keeps the glint sharp — metallic, never bloomed.

04Damping & Decay

Let it cool

Every ripple fades on a Gaussian envelope over two to three seconds, and the pointer dent eases back toward zero. Energy leaves the system fast — the surface returns to hammered stillness, carrying only the memory of the touch.
