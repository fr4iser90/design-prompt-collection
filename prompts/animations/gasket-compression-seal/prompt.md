# Gasket Compression Seal

Create a macro animation demonstrating industrial sealing.

1. **Setup**: Two aluminum plates (top and bottom) with a red rubber O-ring or flat gasket in between.
2. **Motion**: The top plate descends slowly. 
3. **Deformation**: As the plate makes contact, the red gasket squishes outward (bulge effect) at the sides. 
4. **Seal**: When fully compressed, the gasket edges flatten against the plates. A subtle 'lock' indicator (e.g., a green light or checkmark) appears.
5. **Style**: Clean, technical, no grime. Focus on the material stress of the rubber.
