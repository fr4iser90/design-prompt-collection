Method — Shader-driven surface aging

The patina is never painted on. It grows — seeded from random nucleation points, expanded by layered domain-warped noise, its edges displaced and darkened where the corrosion thickens.

Two textures do the work: a procedural brushed-brass base carrying a warm specular lobe, and a time-varying noise field that reveals matte verdigris through a moving smoothstep threshold. Displacement bends the UVs so the boundary wobbles organically rather than dissolving.

As coverage climbs, the lighting rig drifts from a warm studio key toward cool ambient — highlights flatten and spread, occlusion deepens in the crevices, and the metal appears to sink beneath its own history.
