# Oxidized Brass Tarnish

Create a macro close-up animation of a brushed brass plate. 

**Visual Direction:**
- Start with a clean, polished brass surface with sharp specular highlights.
- Animate the onset of oxidation: dark green/teal verdigris blooms from random nucleation points.
- The tarnish should move like thick, viscous fluid or ink on water, not a simple opacity fade.
- Use SVG displacement maps or WebGL shader noise to distort the tarnish edges, giving them organic, dendritic growth patterns.
- Lighting shifts slightly from warm studio light to cooler ambient light as the surface ages.

**Technical Constraints:**
- Avoid linear gradients. Use noise-based color mixing for realism.
- High contrast between the metallic shine and the matte, chalky tarnish.
- Smooth 60fps loop or one-way progression.

**Deliverable:**
HTML/CSS/JS (Canvas/WebGL preferred) or high-fidelity Lottie JSON.
