# Bone Dust Spiral — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
A study of micro-motion in a macro-environment. This animation focuses on the tactile quality of dry, organic matter (bone dust/chalk) reacting to wind. It evokes the silence of the desert broken only by the whisper of sand.

**Palette:**
- **Dust:** Bone White (#e6e2d8), Light Clay (#c4b7a6).
- **Shadowed Dust:** Warm Grey (#a89f91), Dark Stone (#5d5751).
- **Background:** Blurred Sand (#d8d2c5).

**Typography:**
- **Display:** A distressed, textured typeface (e.g., *Trajan Pro* or a custom stencil) that looks like it’s been carved into stone. Color: Dark Brown (#5d5751).
- **Body:** None, or very small, technical labels in a monospace font.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** The spiral is centered, occupying the middle 50% of the screen. Text is placed in the negative space on the left.
- **Mobile:** The spiral is off-center, anchored to the bottom right. Text overlays the top left.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Entrance:** Dust is initially settled at the bottom. A gust kicks it up.
2. **Action:** The particles rise in a helical path. They vary in size, creating depth. Larger particles move slower; smaller ones race up. They collide subtly, changing direction.
3. **Interaction:** Moving the cursor acts as a windbreak, deflecting the spiral. The dust swirls around the cursor's 'presence'.
4. **Loop:** The spiral continues indefinitely, with new particles entering from the bottom as old ones fade out at the top.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] Particles must not be perfect circles.
- [ ] Lighting must show rotation/tumbling.
- [ ] Background must be significantly blurred to create depth of field.
- [ ] Performance must remain smooth at 60fps.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The motion feels natural and wind-driven, not programmed.
- The texture of the 'dust' feels gritty and dry.
- The interaction with the cursor is responsive and playful.
