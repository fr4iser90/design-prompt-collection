# Bone Dust Spiral

Create a particle-based animation simulating a thermal updraft lifting fine, bone-colored dust from a desert surface.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Background:** A soft-focus, gradient background representing a distant dune (color #c4b7a6 to #e6e2d8).
- **Foreground:** Thousands of tiny, irregular particles (representing bone dust/chalk). They are not perfect circles; they are jagged micro-shards.
- **Motion:** The particles rise in a tight, spiraling vortex. They catch the light, flashing briefly as they rotate. The movement is chaotic but guided by the wind current.
- **Atmosphere:** A sense of dryness and weight. The particles should not float like feathers; they should tumble and clump slightly.

**Technical Constraints:**
- Use HTML5 Canvas or WebGL for performance with high particle counts.
- Implement simple physics: gravity pulling down, wind force pushing up and rotating.
- Lighting: Simulate directional light so some particles appear bright white and others in shadow (grey #5d5751).
- The background should remain static and blurry to emphasize the speed of the dust.
