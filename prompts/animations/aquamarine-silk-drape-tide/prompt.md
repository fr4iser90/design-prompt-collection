# Aquamarine Silk Drape Tide

Create a 12-second loop animation of a heavy, semi-translucent aquamarine silk sheet draped over an invisible submerged form. 

**Visual Rules:**
- Material: High-denier silk with a subtle sheen, not satin. Color: Deep saline teal (#0f2b33) transitioning to pale foam (#d0e8eb) at peaks.
- Physics: The fabric moves as if submerged in shallow, slow-moving tide. Ripples propagate horizontally from left to right.
- Lighting: Caustic light refraction appears *inside* the fabric weave, not just on top. Use subsurface scattering to make thin areas glow.
- Reveal: As the fabric lifts, white sans-serif text (Brand: 'SOLIS DEEP') appears in the negative space behind the drape, distorted by the fabric's tension before snapping clear.
- Background: Deep void blue, no gradient noise. Pure depth.

**Motion:**
1. **Entrance:** Fabric settles from stillness into a slow, rhythmic undulation.
2. **Ambient:** Continuous gentle tide ripple (3-4 second cycle).
3. **Interaction:** On hover, the fabric 'tightens' and becomes clearer, revealing the text beneath fully.
