# Glass Buoy Fresnel Focus

Create a macro-style 3D animation of a classic maritime Fresnel lens rotating in slow motion against a backdrop of blurred, deep-sea blue water.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** A complex, concentric-ring glass lens. Realistic refraction indices.
- **Background:** A textured, blue-water surface (caustics) that is heavily blurred (depth of field).
- **Effect:** As the lens rotates, it acts as a lens, bending the background light. At specific angles, the refraction aligns to focus the background blur into a sharp, legible word (e.g., 'CLARITY' or 'DEEP') formed by the distorted caustics.
- **Lighting:** A single, strong light source behind the lens, creating bright, sharp edges on the glass rings.

**Motion:**
1. **Rotation:** Continuous, slow rotation (approx. 10 RPM).
2. **Focus:** The background blur is constant, but the *readability* of the text changes with the lens angle.
3. **Ambient:** Subtle water ripple movement in the background.
