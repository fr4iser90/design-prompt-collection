# Rail Switch Lock Mechanism

**Intent:** Visualize the definitive, irreversible action of a railway switch locking into place.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** Macro view of a mechanical railway point lock. Heavy cast iron components.
- **Material:** Brushed iron, oil-stained steel, and a single bright red locking pin or indicator.
- **Motion:** A linear actuator moves a heavy steel rod into a locking slot. The movement is slow, weighted, and ends with a sharp, abrupt stop (clunk).
- **Lighting:** High-contrast specular highlights on the metal curves. Dark, moody ambient light.
- **Deliverable:** A 3-4 second animation loop or single-shot interaction.

**Constraints:**
- No floating elements; everything must feel anchored by gravity.
- The 'click' moment should be visually represented by a slight compression or highlight flare.
- Colors: Iron Gray, Deep Blue-Gray, Safety Red.
