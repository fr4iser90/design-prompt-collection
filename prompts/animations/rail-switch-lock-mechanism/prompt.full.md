# Rail Switch Lock Mechanism — Extended Brief

## Concept
Railways are systems of absolute certainty. This animation captures the mechanical certainty of a switch lock. It is not about speed, but about the finality of a decision made by infrastructure. The focus is on the materiality of the metal and the physics of heavy objects moving into alignment.

## Palette
- **Primary:** Cast Iron Gray `#4A5568`
- **Accent:** Warning Red `#E53E3E` (Locking Pin/Indicator)
- **Background:** Deep Slate `#2D3748`

## Type & Layout
- **Type:** Optional monospaced label 'LOCKED' appearing after engagement.
- **Layout:** Centered macro shot. Depth of field is shallow, focusing on the contact point of the lock.

## Motion Brief
- **Action:** The steel rod moves from right to left (or vice versa) into the housing.
- **Timing:** Ease-in for start, constant velocity for travel, abrupt ease-out for the lock.
- **Feedback:** Upon locking, a subtle vibration/shake of the entire camera frame to simulate the impact. A red indicator slides into view or lights up.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] Metal surfaces must show texture (brush marks, oil sheen).
- [ ] Motion must feel heavy (high inertia).
- [ ] The locking sound cue (visualized) must be precise.
- [ ] No purple/blue glow effects.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The mechanism feels functional and real.
- The red accent draws the eye to the critical state change.
- The animation conveys 'safety through rigidity'.
