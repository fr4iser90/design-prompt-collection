# Molten Glass Pour

Create a slow-motion animation of viscous molten glass flowing from a source.

**Visual Direction:**
- The glass should move like honey or thick syrup, not water.
- Color gradient: Bright white-hot core at the source, fading to amber/yellow, then to transparent cool glass at the bottom.
- Heavy refraction: Objects behind the flow should be distorted and magnified.
- Thermal glow: The glass emits its own light (emissive material) that fades as it cools.

**Technical Constraints:**
- Use fluid simulation with high viscosity parameters.
- Implement realistic refraction (IOR > 1.5).
- Bloom effect on the hottest parts.
- Smooth, continuous flow without breaks.

**Deliverable:**
Three.js/WebGL scene with custom shaders for refraction and emission.
