# Hammered Tin Hull

Animate the process of hammering a flat sheet of tin into a curved shape.

**Visuals:**
- Material: Soft, dull silver tin. Not steel; it should deform easily.
- Action: A hammer strikes the surface, creating a localized dent. The metal stretches and curves around the impact point.
- Lighting: Soft, diffused environment light reflecting off the growing curvature.
- Progression: Show multiple strikes building up the form. The surface becomes a mosaic of small facets.

**Constraints:**
- Focus on the *deformation* of the metal, not just the hammer motion.
- Realistic metal flow: material moves to the edges as it's stretched.
- No sparks, no heat. Just cold working.
