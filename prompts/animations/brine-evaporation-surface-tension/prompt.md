# Brine Evaporation Surface Tension

Create a macro animation of a pool of saline water evaporating on a glass surface.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Perspective:** Extreme macro, top-down or slight angle. Shallow depth of field.
- **Palette:** High-key. Whites, pale blues, grays. Clean, clinical, 'lab' aesthetic.
- **Subject:** The water edge (meniscus) is sharp and trembling. As water recedes, salt crystals nucleate and grow outward in fractal patterns.
- **Lighting:** Diffused, cool white light. Hard shadows for the crystals to emphasize their 3D geometry.
- **Motion:** Slow, time-lapse feel. The water level drops subtly. Crystals grow in 'pops' or rapid bursts, then freeze.
- **Deliverable:** GIF or MP4, 1080x1080, focus on the edge where water meets salt.
