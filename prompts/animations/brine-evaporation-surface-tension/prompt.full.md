# Brine Evaporation Surface Tension — Extended

**Concept:**
The moment of transition from liquid chaos to solid order. This animation explores the physics of evaporation and crystallization as a metaphor for clarity and refinement. The 'saline' palette evokes tears, sea, and preservation. The focus is on the *interface*—the boundary between states.

**Palette:**
- **Pure White:** #ffffff
- **Pale Saline Blue:** #e6f0f2
- **Glass Reflection:** #b0c4de
- **Salt Crystal Shadow:** #8899a6
- **Deep Shadow:** #4a5568 (for depth)

**Type & Layout:**
Minimalist. If text is present, it is small, sans-serif, and placed in the negative space of the white background, never obscuring the crystal formation.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **State 1:** Pool of water, surface rippling gently.
2. **Transition:** Water level drops. The meniscus clings to the glass, stretching.
3. **Event:** Nucleation. A tiny crystal forms. It grows rapidly, branching out. The water line recedes around it.
4. **State 2:** The crystal is fully formed, sharp, and dry. The remaining water is a thin film.

**Constraints:**
- Avoid 'magic wand' sparkle effects. Crystals must look scientifically accurate (cubic or dendritic).
- Lighting must be consistent. No color shifts.
- The 'wet' look of the water must be distinct from the 'dry' look of the salt.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The surface tension of the water is visible (curved meniscus).
- The crystal growth feels organic but geometrically precise.
- The palette is strictly cool and clean, avoiding warm tones.
