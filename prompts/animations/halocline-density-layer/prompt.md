# Halocline Density Layer

Generate an abstract, scientific-art animation of ocean stratification. Two distinct fluid layers (fresh water above, salt water below) meet at a sharp, shifting boundary (the halocline).

**Visual Rules:**
- **Layers:** Top layer is pale, turbid blue (#7fb6c9). Bottom layer is deep, clear navy (#1a2a32). 
- **Boundary:** The interface between them is not a line, but a chaotic, billowing zone of mixing (Kelvin-Helmholtz instabilities) that moves slowly.
- **Data Integration:** Glowing, thin lines of white light (#e0f2f5) descend from the top layer, hit the density boundary, and refract or bounce back, visualizing 'signal vs. noise' or 'entry vs. barrier'.
- **Lighting:** Top-down sunlight penetrating the upper layer, dimming rapidly as it enters the lower layer.

**Motion:**
1. **Ambient:** Slow, organic churning of the boundary layer.
2. **Action:** A 'pulse' of light data drops every 3 seconds, interacting with the boundary.
3. **Style:** Clean, medical/scientific, not chaotic. High contrast between the turbid top and clear bottom.
