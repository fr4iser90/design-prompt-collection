# Sun-Bleached Rope Tension

**Concept:** A hyper-realistic, macro view of a thick hemp rope pulled taut. The focus is on the friction between fibers and the 'bleached' quality of old material.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** A single strand of twisted rope, centered vertically or horizontally.
- **Texture:** High-detail fiber simulation. Individual threads fray and separate slightly under tension. Color is uneven—sun-bleached white on the exposed side, darker beige on the shadow side.
- **Motion:** The rope 'settles' into tension. Fibers twitch and snap into place. A subtle vibration travels along the length.
- **Lighting:** Direct, overhead sunlight. Hard shadows between the twists of the rope. No soft diffuse light.
- **Palette:** Bleached hemp (#e0d6c8), shadowed fiber (#a89f91), highlight (#f5f0e6).

**Deliverable:** A WebGL or CSS 3D transform animation where the rope tightens, fibers splay, and light plays across the textured surface.
