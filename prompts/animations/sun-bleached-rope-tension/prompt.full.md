# Sun-Bleached Rope Tension — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
Explore the material history of 'sun-bleached' objects. The rope is not new; it has been exposed to the elements. The animation captures the moment of tension application, revealing the internal structure of the weave. It’s a study of friction, age, and strength in an arid context.

**Palette:**
- **Bleached Fiber:** #e0d6c8 (The primary exposed color)
- **Deep Fiber:** #a89f91 (The unexposed, shadowed core)
- **Highlight:** #f5f0e6 (The peak of the sun-bleached thread)
- **Background:** #ffffff (Pure white to isolate the rope) or very light sand #f7f4ef.

**Typography & UI:**
- None. This is a pure material study.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** The rope runs diagonally across the screen. As the user scrolls or hovers, the tension increases, causing more fibers to splay outward.
- **Mobile:** Horizontal rope across the center. Tap to 'snap' a loose fiber into place.

**Motion Brief:**
1.  **Entrance:** Rope is slack, twisted loosely. Fibers are hidden within the twist.
2.  **Ambient:** A constant, very slight sway (wind), but the primary motion is 'settling'.
3.  **Interaction:** On hover/click, the rope pulls taut. The twist tightens. Sun-bleached outer fibers pop out, catching the light. A 'creak' visual effect (subtle jitter) accompanies the tightening.

**Constraints:**
- No synthetic materials (nylon/plastic).
- No wet look. The rope must look dry and dusty.
- Lighting must be harsh, not soft.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Individual fibers must be distinguishable in the texture map.
- The color gradient from bleached to shadowed must be sharp.
- The motion should feel 'heavy' and 'resistant', not elastic or rubbery.
