# Ink Brush Lifting

Capture the micro-interaction of a calligraphy brush lifting off textured paper.

**Visuals:**
- Material: Deep black ink, fibrous cream paper, wet bristles.
- Action: The brush tip is pressed down, then slowly lifts. A thin strand of ink connects the brush to the paper before snapping.
- Detail: The paper fibers absorb the ink, creating a soft, fuzzy edge. The ink strand stretches and vibrates slightly before breaking.
- Perspective: Extreme macro, side-on view.

**Constraints:**
- No UI. Pure observational footage style.
- Focus on the tension and release of the liquid.
- The paper must look dry and matte; the ink must look wet and glossy.
