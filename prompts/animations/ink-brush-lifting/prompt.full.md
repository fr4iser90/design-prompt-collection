# Ink Brush Lifting — Extended

**Concept:**
A study of liquid dynamics and traditional art materials. This animation isolates the most delicate moment in calligraphy: the lift. It highlights the viscosity of ink, the absorbency of paper, and the tension of the liquid bridge.

**Art Direction:**
- **Palette:** Stark contrast between deep ink black (#0f0f0f) and warm, unbleached paper (#f5f2eb).
- **Texture:** Highly detailed paper grain. The ink should have a slight gloss and reflect the light source.
- **Lighting:** Soft, directional light from the side to highlight the meniscus of the ink and the shadows of the paper fibers.

**Motion Design:**
1.  **Press:** The brush is already on the paper, ink pooling slightly at the contact point.
2.  **Lift Initiation:** The brush moves upward slowly. The ink surface curves into a cone.
3.  **Strand Formation:** A thin thread of ink remains attached to the paper as the brush rises. The thread vibrates subtly.
4.  **Break:** The strand snaps due to surface tension. The ink recoils slightly back into the paper or onto the brush.
5.  **Settle:** The paper absorbs the remaining ink, darkening slightly. The scene ends with the completed stroke.

**Technical Notes:**
- Use fluid simulation with high viscosity and low Reynolds number.
- Surface tension effects are critical for the strand breaking.
- Render with subsurface scattering for the paper to show ink absorption.
