# Sun-Cracked Mud Heal — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
A meditative animation about restoration and the cycle of water. By reversing the natural decay of drying mud, we create a sense of healing and return to vitality. It contrasts the harshness of the arid environment with the softness of wet earth.

**Palette:**
- **Dry Mud:** Dusty Ochre (#a39d91), Warm Taupe (#8c8577).
- **Cracks:** Deep Charcoal (#4a443d).
- **Wet Earth:** Slightly darker, more saturated browns (#6e665b).

**Typography:**
- **Display:** A rounded, soft sans-serif (e.g., *Nunito* or *Quicksand*) to contrast with the jagged cracks. Color: White (#f4f1ea) with a soft glow.
- **Body:** A clean, minimal sans-serif in dark grey.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** Full-bleed texture. Text is centered, fading in and out in rhythm with the 'breathing' of the mud.
- **Mobile:** The texture is cropped to a square or portrait aspect ratio. Text is bottom-aligned.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Entrance:** The screen shows fully cracked, dry earth. Heat haze is visible.
2. **Action:** The cracks begin to seal from the edges inward. The 'plates' of mud expand slightly to meet each other. The surface gains a subtle wet sheen.
3. **Interaction:** Clicking triggers the healing process. Hovering over a specific crack causes it to seal locally.
4. **Loop:** After fully healing, the mud slowly dries out again, reversing the process to restart the cycle.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] The 'healing' effect must look like fluid smoothing, not just a fade.
- [ ] Lighting must change realistically as the surface becomes wet.
- [ ] The texture must be high-resolution to avoid pixelation on large screens.
- [ ] No 'digital' effects; keep it organic and material.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The animation feels satisfying and calming.
- The transition between dry and wet states is visually distinct but seamless.
- The interaction feels tactile, like pressing your finger into wet clay.
