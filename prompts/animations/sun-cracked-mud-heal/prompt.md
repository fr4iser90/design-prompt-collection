# Sun-Cracked Mud Heal

Create an animation that plays in reverse: showing dry, cracked mud earth smoothing over and sealing its fissures.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Texture:** High-fidelity texture of dry mud plates (color #a39d91) separated by deep, dark cracks (#4a443d).
- **Action:** The cracks widen initially (to show the dry state), then begin to close. As they close, the edges soften, blurring into each other. The surface transitions from matte/dry to a slightly wet/sheen finish.
- **Lighting:** Low-angle desert sun, emphasizing the topography of the cracks. As the cracks heal, the shadows shorten and disappear.
- **Atmosphere:** A sense of relief and restoration. The visual is hypnotic and soothing.

**Technical Constraints:**
- Use displacement maps or shader-based vertex manipulation to simulate the closing of gaps.
- The transition from 'matte' to 'wet' should be subtle, achieved via specular highlights.
- The motion should be slow and deliberate, not instant.
