# Vellum Fold Mechanism

Create a high-fidelity animation of archival vellum being folded along a precise score line.

**Visuals:**
- Material: Heavy, matte archival vellum (warm off-white, slight grain).
- Lighting: Soft, diffuse north light. No harsh speculars.
- Action: The paper folds slowly (4-second duration). At the crease, the paper compresses microscopically, creating a darker shadow line that sharpens as the fold completes.
- Reveal: As the flap closes, it covers a bright, crisp line of text underneath, leaving only a faint indentation visible.

**Motion:**
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1.0)` for a heavy, deliberate feel.
- Physics: Simulate slight stiffness in the paper away from the fold. No floppy edges.

**Deliverable:**
- Clean SVG/HTML animation.
- Focus on the tactile quality of the paper edge and the shadow cast by the fold.
