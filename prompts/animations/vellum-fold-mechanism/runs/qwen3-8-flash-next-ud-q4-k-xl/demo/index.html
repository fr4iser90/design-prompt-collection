01 / Principles

Structure as trust

No glitches. No flash. Only the weight of paper and the precision of the standard.

A — MATERIAL

Heavy matte vellum

Warm off-white stock with visible fibre. The edge holds a crisp, tactile line — never floppy, never tissue-light.

B — LIGHTING

Diffuse north light

A large, soft source from the top-left. Shadows are defined but never harsh; the curvature of the fold reads through gradient alone.

C — THE CREASE

Compression sharpened

Pressure at the score line compresses the fibre. A shadow deepens and tightens as the fold completes — a permanent record of the bend.

D — THE REVEAL

Indentation, not erasure

The flap covers the crisp text beneath, leaving only a faint debossed shadow — proof the record was here, filed, and sealed.

02 / Specification

The numbers behind the weight

Motion is expensive when it is deliberate. Every degree of the fold is measured against the standard it preserves. Easing is asymmetric by design — the sheet accelerates under its own weight, then settles with an imperceptible micro-bounce into the crease.

03 / Process

Five moments of the fold

1
00:00 — Rest

Two sheets, slightly overlapping

The vellum lies flat under even light. Nothing moves. The score line is invisible until it is needed.

2
00:00.4 — Initiation

Pressure along the score

A soft shadow gathers at the crease as force is applied. The fibre begins to compress before the sheet ever moves.

3
00:01.2 — The fold

Rotation under its own weight

The top half descends. As the angle changes, the surface gradient shifts — light slides across the curvature of the bend.

4
00:03.6 — Settling

A micro-bounce, felt not seen

The paper lands. The shadow line deepens into a permanent crease, sharpening as the fold resolves to its final form.

5
00:04.0 — Sealed

Only the indentation remains

The flap has covered the record. What survives is a faint debossed shadow — the quiet proof of preservation.
