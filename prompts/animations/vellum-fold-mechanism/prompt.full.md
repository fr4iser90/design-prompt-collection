# Vellum Fold Mechanism — Extended

**Concept**
Trust is built on structure and permanence. This animation visualizes the physical act of filing away a record—folding a document into its archival envelope. It emphasizes the weight of the paper and the precision of the archival standard. No digital glitches, no flashy transitions; just the quiet dignity of preservation.

**Art Direction**
- **Palette:** Bone white (#F5F2EB), Shadow grey (#D4CFC4), Indentation dark (#8A857D).
- **Texture:** High-res paper grain. Subtle fibers visible at macro zoom.
- **Lighting:** Simulate a large, diffuse light source from the top-left (North light). Shadows should be soft but defined, emphasizing the 3D curvature of the fold.

**Layout**
- **Desktop:** Centered viewport. The fold happens along the vertical center line.
- **Mobile:** The fold axis is horizontal, adapting to the narrower width.

**Motion Brief**
1. **Rest:** Two sheets of vellum lie flat, slightly overlapping.
2. **Initiation:** A subtle shadow appears along the score line as pressure is applied.
3. **The Fold:** The top half rotates down. As it moves, the surface curvature changes, shifting the light gradient.
4. **Settling:** The paper lands with a micro-bounce (imperceptible to the eye, felt in the easing curve). The shadow line deepens into a permanent crease.
5. **Ambient:** Slow, breathing dust motes in the light beam (optional, very subtle).

**Constraints**
- No purple glow or neon accents.
- No fast, bouncy 'cartoon' physics.
- The paper must feel heavy and stiff, not like tissue.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- The shadow must accurately reflect the changing angle of the paper.
- The texture must remain crisp during transform.
- The motion must feel expensive and deliberate.
