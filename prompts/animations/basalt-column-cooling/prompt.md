# Basalt Column Cooling

**Concept:** A macro, top-down animation of hot basalt solidifying and cracking into geometric columns.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** Dark gray/black basalt surface transitioning to cooler, lighter stone tones.
- **Motion:** Hexagonal crack lines propagate outward from cooling centers. The 'crack' is not violent but a slow, structural settling.
- **Lighting:** Harsh, directional sunlight casting long, sharp shadows into the newly formed fissures. Dust motes float in the heat haze.
- **Palette:** Stone gray (#3a3a38), bone white dust (#d4cfc4), warm desert light (#f2eadd).
- **Atmosphere:** Arid, quiet, geological time compressed.

**Deliverable:** A looping SVG/CSS animation or WebGL shader where cracks grow and shadows deepen as the color shifts from hot-dark to cool-light.
