# Salt Pan Crystallization

Create a seamless loop animation depicting the rapid crystallization of salt from shallow water in a desert pan.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Background:** A flat, reflective surface of shallow brine (color #f4f1ea) with a subtle, static gradient indicating depth.
- **Action:** White, geometric salt crystals (spiky, cubic-like structures) erupt from the liquid surface in a staggered, organic but rigid manner.
- **Lighting:** Harsh, overhead desert sun. Cast sharp, short shadows from the newly formed crystals onto the wet surface. The water reflects the sky (pale blue-grey) and the white crystals.
- **Atmosphere:** Heat haze distortion at the horizon line, blurring the background slightly. No particles floating in the air, only the static heat shimmer.
- **Motion:** The crystals grow and shift slightly, pushing each other aside. The water recedes visibly (or the crystal coverage increases). The motion should be snappy but weighty, mimicking the sudden snap of crystalline growth.

**Technical Constraints:**
- Use CSS transforms for crystal scaling/rotation.
- Use `backdrop-filter` or SVG filters for the heat haze.
- Ensure the loop is seamless: crystals do not disappear, they just stop growing and the camera drifts slightly to reset.
