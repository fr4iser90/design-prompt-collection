# Salt Pan Crystallization — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
This animation captures the intense, quiet violence of evaporation in an arid environment. It focuses on the transition from liquid to solid, emphasizing the geometric inevitability of salt crystal formation. The mood is stark, blindingly bright, and silent.

**Palette:**
- **Core:** Bone White (#f4f1ea), Pale Sand (#d8d2c5), Wet Slate (#bcaaa4).
- **Shadow:** Deep Umber (#8d6e63) — used sparingly for sharp cast shadows.
- **Accent:** None. The color palette is monochromatic in hue, varying only in value and saturation to convey light and moisture.

**Typography:**
- **Display:** A high-contrast serif with sharp serifs (e.g., *Didot* or *Playfair Display*) to mirror the sharp edges of the crystals. White text with a subtle black drop shadow for readability against the bright background.
- **Body:** A clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Futura* or *Century Gothic*) in dark grey (#333).

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** Full-viewport animation. Title centered, low opacity, blending into the background. A small, static credit in the bottom right corner.
- **Mobile:** Vertical crop of the animation. Title overlaid in the top third.

**Motion Brief:**
1. **Entrance:** The water surface is calm. A faint ripple disturbs the surface.
2. **Action:** Salt crystals begin to nucleate and grow. They start as small dots and expand into spiky, white structures. The growth is not uniform; some clusters grow faster, creating a rugged, textured terrain.
3. **Interaction:** If the user hovers, the heat haze intensifies, distorting the view as if the screen is melting slightly.
4. **Loop:** The camera pans slowly to the right, revealing a new section of the pan that looks identical, creating an infinite desert.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] No colorful gradients. Keep it desaturated.
- [ ] Shadows must be sharp and distinct (hard light source).
- [ ] The motion must feel physical and rigid, not floaty.
- [ ] Ensure high contrast for text accessibility.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The animation loops seamlessly without visible jumps.
- The crystallization process feels organic yet geometrically precise.
- The 'heat haze' effect is subtle and atmospheric, not distracting.
