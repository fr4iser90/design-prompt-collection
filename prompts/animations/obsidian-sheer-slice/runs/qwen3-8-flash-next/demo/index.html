Notes

Obsidian is volcanic glass quenched so rapidly that its atoms never arrange into a crystal lattice. With no grain to follow, it breaks the way a soap bubble does — along smooth, curved, shell-like surfaces.

The fracture front propagates faster than the eye can resolve. Rendered at half speed, each ripple becomes legible: a fingerprint left by light trapped mid-collapse.

There is no ambient fill. Shadows fall to absolute black so the specular edge — a knife-thin thread of white — reads as the only truth on the surface.
