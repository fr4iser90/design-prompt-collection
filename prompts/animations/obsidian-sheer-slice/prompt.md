# Obsidian Sheer Slice

Create a macro animation of a block of obsidian (volcanic glass) fracturing. 

**Visuals:**
- Material: Pure black, glassy, conchoidal fracture patterns.
- Lighting: Single hard key light from top-left to create sharp white specular highlights on the sharp edges.
- Action: A stress line appears, then the surface splits along curved, shell-like paths.
- Detail: As the pieces separate slightly, the interior reveals a faint, deep-red thermal glow (cooling lava) before settling to black.

**Constraints:**
- No UI overlays.
- Focus on the sharpness of the break and the reflective quality of the glass.
- Smooth, slow-motion physics for the separation.
