# Substation Warning Flag Drop — Extended Brief

## Concept
Capture the visceral sense of danger and caution inherent in utility infrastructure. The animation focuses on the moment a safety flag is deployed and begins to interact with the wind. It is not graceful; it is urgent and physical. The contrast between the rigid, cold steel pole and the vibrant, chaotic orange fabric creates visual tension.

## Palette
- **Primary:** Safety Orange `#FF5733` (Flag)
- **Secondary:** Galvanized Steel Gray `#C0C0C0` (Pole/Hardware)
- **Background:** Deep Shadow/Asphalt `#1A1A1A` (Ambient)

## Type & Layout
- **Type:** None. This is a pure motion study.
- **Layout:** Centered composition. The pole is a vertical anchor on the left or right third, allowing the flag to sweep across the negative space.

## Motion Brief
- **Entrance:** The flag snaps into place from a folded state (or fades in already deployed).
- **Ambient:** Continuous wind simulation. Use a combination of sine waves for base rhythm and noise for turbulence. The fabric should stretch and compress slightly to sell the velocity.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over the pole increases wind speed temporarily, causing the flag to snap taut.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] Fabric thickness must be visible (edge highlights).
- [ ] Motion must loop seamlessly.
- [ ] Lighting must be consistent (single hard source).
- [ ] No cartoonish squash-and-stretch; realistic cloth physics.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The flag feels heavy, not like tissue paper.
- The orange is vibrant against the dark background.
- The animation evokes a sense of caution and active work zones.
