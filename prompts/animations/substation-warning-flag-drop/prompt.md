# Substation Warning Flag Drop

**Intent:** Simulate the physical tension of a high-visibility safety flag in a gale.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** A triangular or rectangular orange warning flag attached to a gray, galvanized steel utility pole.
- **Material:** Heavy-duty synthetic mesh with visible weave texture; stiff edges that resist bending.
- **Motion:** Continuous, irregular flapping driven by Perlin noise wind simulation. The flag should exhibit secondary motion (tip lags behind root).
- **Lighting:** Hard, directional sunlight creating sharp shadows of the flag folds onto the pole.
- **Background:** Blurred, out-of-focus substation equipment or power lines to maintain depth without distraction.
- **Deliverable:** Seamless loop animation (5-8 seconds) focusing on the flag's chaotic but rhythmic movement.

**Constraints:**
- No smooth easing; motion must feel turbulent and heavy.
- Flag color must be exact safety orange (#FF5733).
- Pole must show weathering (dust, minor rust spots).
