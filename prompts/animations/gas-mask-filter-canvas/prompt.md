# Gas Mask Filter Canvas

**Intent:** A tactile, technical study of industrial safety gear materials.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** Circular or hexagonal gas mask filter face. Focus on the central mesh/canvas area.
- **Material:** Coarse woven fabric or activated carbon mesh. Visible texture, slight discoloration from use.
- **Motion:** Subtle, breathing-like expansion and contraction of the mesh as air is drawn through. Tiny particles (dust) are trapped on the surface.
- **Lighting:** Cool, sterile overhead light contrasting with the warm, dusty tones of the filter material.
- **Deliverable:** Seamless loop (4 seconds) of the 'breathing' filter.

**Constraints:**
- The mesh deformation should be subtle, not exaggerated.
- Particles should stick, not bounce off.
- Colors: Industrial Charcoal, Dust Gray, Rust/Copper accents.
