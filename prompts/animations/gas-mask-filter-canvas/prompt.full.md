# Gas Mask Filter Canvas — Extended Brief

## Concept
Safety gear is often hidden, but its function is critical. This animation brings attention to the microscopic work happening in a gas mask filter. It visualizes the 'effort' of filtering, turning a static object into a dynamic, living shield. The aesthetic is gritty and utilitarian.

## Palette
- **Primary:** Industrial Charcoal `#2C3E50` (Housing)
- **Secondary:** Dust Gray `#95A5A6` (Mesh/Fabric)
- **Accent:** Rust/Copper `#D35400` (Contamination/Particulate)

## Type & Layout
- **Type:** Small, stamped serial number visible on the rim.
- **Layout:** Perfectly centered, circular composition. Fills the frame.

## Motion Brief
- **Ambient:** The filter 'breathes.' The mesh center dips slightly inward and bulges outward rhythmically.
- **Particle System:** Small dark specks drift toward the center and stick to the mesh, accumulating slightly.
- **Lighting:** Light shifts subtly as the mesh deforms, creating changing highlights on the weave.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] Mesh texture must be high-resolution and realistic.
- [ ] Breathing motion must be rhythmic but not robotic.
- [ ] Particles must look like dust/soot, not glitter.
- [ ] No sci-fi glow; realistic optical properties.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The filter looks used and functional.
- The motion conveys the passage of air.
- The overall mood is serious and protective.
