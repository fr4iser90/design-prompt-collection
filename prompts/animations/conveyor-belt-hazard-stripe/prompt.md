# Conveyor Belt Hazard Stripe

**Goal:** Create a hypnotic, high-contrast animation of industrial movement and detection.

**Visuals:**
- **Background:** A dark, matte black canvas (#1A1A1A) representing the factory floor.
- **Conveyor Belt:** A wide, black rubber belt moves diagonally from bottom-left to top-right. It has a subtle, ribbed texture.
- **Hazard Marking:** A thick, bright yellow (#FFD700) stripe runs parallel to the belt edges but moves *with* the belt. 
- **Scanner Gate:** A fixed, silver-gray (#808080) archway in the foreground. A thin red laser line shoots across the belt from the gate.
- **Action:** 
  1. The belt moves continuously at a steady pace.
  2. As the yellow stripe passes under the laser, the laser line 'bounces' or refracts slightly (a visual cue of detection).
  3. A small digital counter on the gate increments '1' every time a stripe passes.

**Motion:**
- Linear infinite scroll for the belt and stripes.
- The 'bounce' of the laser should be a quick, elastic easing (spring) when the stripe hits the beam.
- The counter increment should be a sharp, digital flicker (no smooth transition).

**Constraints:**
- The motion must be perfectly seamless for a loop.
- No purple/neon aesthetics. Stick to industrial safety colors (Yellow/Black/Red).
- The texture of the belt should be visible but not distracting (low contrast noise).
