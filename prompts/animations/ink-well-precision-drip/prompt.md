# Ink Well Precision Drip

Create a macro animation of an ink drop forming and falling.

**Visuals:**
- Object: Glass dip pen tip (top right) and a shallow ink well (center bottom).
- Fluid: High-viscosity black ink. Reflective surface, deep black.
- Action:
  1. Ink swells at the pen tip.
  2. Drop separates and falls (slow motion).
  3. Impact creates a concentric ripple in the well.
  4. The ripple distortion briefly reveals letters 'INDEX' in negative space before settling.

**Motion:**
- Physics: Realistic surface tension. The drop should elongate before breaking.
- Easing: Slow, dreamlike speed (10x slow motion).

**Deliverable:**
- Canvas or WebGL fluid simulation (or high-quality SVG path animation if fluid is too complex).
- Focus on the refraction of the ink and the clarity of the resulting text.
