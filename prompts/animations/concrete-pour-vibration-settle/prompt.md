# Concrete Pour Vibration Settle

**Intent**: Visualize the transition from fluid slurry to solid structure. Focus on material weight, particle settling, and the expulsion of air.

**Visuals**:
- **Subject**: A rectangular wooden formwork (light pine) filled with wet grey concrete.
- **Elements**: Two black steel rebar rods inserted vertically. Small white air bubbles rising to the surface.
- **Surface**: Starts rough, lumpy, and wet. Ends smooth, matte, and slightly dusty.
- **Background**: Dark, industrial void (#2b2b2b) to highlight the grey tones.

**Motion Logic**:
1. **Vibration**: The two rebar rods vibrate rapidly (high frequency, low amplitude). 
2. **Settling**: The concrete surface flattens. Lumps dissolve. The level drops slightly as air escapes.
3. **Bubbles**: Air bubbles rise quickly near the rods, pop, and disappear. 
4. **Hardening**: As vibration stops, the surface sheen shifts from 'wet glossy' to 'dry matte'. A subtle dust layer settles on top.

**Deliverable**: A 3–5 second loop or one-shot animation that clearly shows the 'curing' process through motion.
