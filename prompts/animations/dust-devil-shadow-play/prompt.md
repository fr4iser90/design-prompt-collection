# Dust Devil Shadow Play

**Concept:** We do not see the dust devil itself, only its shadow. A flat, vast, bone-colored desert plane. A soft, elongated shadow moves across it, indicating the presence of the wind vortex.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Subject:** The shadow of a swirling column of dust. The shadow is soft-edged but distinct.
- **Background:** A uniform, textured plane of pale sand/bone (#dcd4c8) with subtle grain.
- **Motion:** The shadow moves from left to right, stretching long in the morning light, then shortening. It wobbles organically.
- **Lighting:** Low-angle sun (long shadows). The shadow is gray-brown (#5c5854), not black. The ground is bright (#f0e9e0).
- **Atmosphere:** Silent, vast, empty.

**Deliverable:** A 2D canvas or SVG animation where a blurred, morphing shadow moves across a textured background. Optional: slight dust particles rising in the wake of the shadow.
