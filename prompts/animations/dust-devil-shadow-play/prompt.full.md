# Dust Devil Shadow Play — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
A study of negative space and atmosphere. The 'object' is invisible; only its effect (the shadow) is visible. This captures the eerie, silent power of desert weather. The composition is sparse, emphasizing the vastness of the ground plane.

**Palette:**
- **Ground:** #dcd4c8 (Pale sand/bone)
- **Shadow:** #5c5854 (Soft gray-brown)
- **Highlight:** #f0e9e0 (Sunlit sand grain)
- **Accent:** #8a8278 (Distant horizon/dust haze)

**Typography & UI:**
- Minimal. A single, thin line indicating 'WIND SPEED' or 'TIME' in the corner, using a monospace font like 'Courier Prime' or 'Space Mono', in dark gray.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** Full-screen landscape. The shadow moves slowly across the screen. The user can 'speed up' the shadow with a scroll gesture.
- **Mobile:** Vertical crop. The shadow passes through the center of the screen. Interaction: Tap to pause the wind, causing the shadow to shrink and fade.

**Motion Brief:**
1.  **Entrance:** The shadow enters from the left edge, distorted by perspective.
2.  **Ambient:** The shadow 'wobbles' and changes shape slightly as it moves, mimicking the turbulent nature of a dust devil. The ground texture subtly shifts (sand grain moving) in the wake of the shadow.
3.  **Interaction:** Scrolling changes the speed and length of the shadow (simulating time of day). Fast scroll = short shadow (noon); Slow scroll = long shadow (dawn).

**Constraints:**
- The dust devil itself must NEVER be drawn. Only the shadow.
- The shadow must not be hard-edged; it is soft and diffused.
- The background must remain relatively flat and sparse.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The shadow motion must feel organic and unpredictable, not linear.
- The contrast between the pale ground and the soft shadow must be subtle but visible.
- The overall mood is calm, desolate, and minimalist.
