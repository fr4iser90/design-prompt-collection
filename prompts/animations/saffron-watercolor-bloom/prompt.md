# Saffron Watercolor Bloom

Create a high-fidelity macro animation of saffron threads releasing pigment into clear water.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** Pure, sterile white (#FFFDFB) to emphasize the purity of the medium. No grime, no rustic bowls.
- **Subject:** 3-5 dried saffron threads entering from the top or side. They should look brittle and dark red initially.
- **Fluid Dynamics:** The pigment release must be complex. Use a fluid simulation style where red streaks branch out into delicate, fractal-like tendrils. Do not use simple opacity fades.
- **Color Shift:** As the water saturates, the color should shift from bright crimson to deep amber/gold (#FFC72C) in the denser areas.
- **Lighting:** High-key studio lighting. Caustics (light refraction through water) should be visible as subtle wavy patterns on the bottom.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Threads fall slowly, hitting the surface with a slight ripple.
- **Ambient:** The diffusion is slow, elegant, and continuous. Particles drift with simulated water currents.
- **Interaction:** Cursor acts as a gentle stirrer, creating vortices in the pigment clouds.

**Deliverable:** A seamless loop or a 5-second cinematic intro.
