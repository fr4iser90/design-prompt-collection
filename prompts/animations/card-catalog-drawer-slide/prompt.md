# Card Catalog Drawer Slide

Animate a classic wooden library card catalog drawer opening and closing.

**Visuals:**
- Material: Dark stained wood (mahogany/walnut) with visible grain. Brass handle (matte, aged).
- Interior: Rows of cream-colored index cards, tightly packed but slightly fanned to show depth.
- Action: Drawer slides out 20%, pauses, then slides back in.
- Highlight: One card in the middle row subtly illuminates from within (warm glow) during the open state.

**Motion:**
- Easing: Smooth mechanical glide. Start slow, accelerate, then decelerate heavily on stop.
- Sound (if supported): Soft wood-on-wood slide, no rattle.

**Deliverable:**
- CSS/JS animation or SVG with motion paths.
- Focus on the depth of the card stack and the smoothness of the drawer rails.
