# Card Catalog Drawer Slide — Extended

**Concept**
The digital search bar is a metaphor for this physical object. This animation restores the tactile satisfaction of finding information. It’s not just about opening a box; it’s about the revelation of organized knowledge. The 'glow' represents the digital index pointing to the physical artifact.

**Art Direction**
- **Palette:** Rich wood tones (#3E2C23 to #8B5A2B), warm card cream (#F0E6D2), muted brass (#B08D57).
- **Lighting:** Top-down ambient light. Inside the drawer, shadows deepen towards the back, creating a sense of depth and volume.
- **Details:** The cards should not be flat rectangles; they need slight perspective distortion to look like a stack. The wood grain should move with the drawer face.

**Layout**
- **Desktop:** Horizontal orientation. Drawer fills the lower half of the screen.
- **Mobile:** Vertical orientation or scaled down horizontal. Touch-friendly interaction area.

**Motion Brief**
1. **Rest:** Drawer fully closed. Flush with the cabinet frame.
2. **Open:** Drawer slides forward. The front face remains sharp; the interior cards reveal themselves gradually. The 'active' card begins its glow fade-in.
3. **Hold:** Brief pause (1s) at 20% open. The glow pulses once.
4. **Close:** Drawer slides back. The cards disappear into shadow. The glow fades out before the drawer closes completely.

**Constraints**
- No bright white cards; they must be aged cream.
- The drawer must not float; it needs to feel anchored to the cabinet.
- No flashy particle effects.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- The perspective shift of the cards as the drawer opens must be convincing.
- The lighting change inside the drawer (from shadow to light) must be smooth.
- The loop must be seamless.
