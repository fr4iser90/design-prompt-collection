# Stainless Brushed Light Sweep

Animate a macro view of a brushed stainless steel panel reacting to a moving light source.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Material:** High-quality brushed metal. The brush marks are fine, horizontal lines (grain direction). 
- **Reflection Model:** Anisotropic. As the light moves vertically, the highlight should stretch and compress horizontally along the grain.
- **Background:** Neutral dark grey or off-white to keep focus on the metal.
- **Lighting:** A single, narrow, bright light source (like a laser or a thin bar light) moving across the surface.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Ambient:** The light source moves slowly and smoothly across the surface, left to right or top to bottom.
- **Interaction:** Cursor position controls the light source. Moving the mouse 'scrubs' the light across the metal, revealing the texture in real-time.
- **Physics:** No deformation of the metal. It is rigid. Only the lighting and reflection change.

**Deliverable:** Interactive component for a hero section or brand intro.
