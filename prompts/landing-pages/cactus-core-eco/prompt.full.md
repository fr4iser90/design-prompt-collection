# Cactus Core Eco — Extended

## Concept
Cactus Core is an eco-conscious brand creating products from cactus leaves. The landing page must highlight sustainability, natural beauty, and texture. The design language is 'arid-minimal' but with an organic, life-focused twist.

## Palette
- **Clay**: #D3C5B5 (Background)
- **Cactus-Green**: #5C7F56 (Accents, Buttons, Icons)
- **Dark Grey**: #333333 (Text)
- **Light Clay**: #E6DCCF (Hover states, subtle backgrounds)

## Typography
- **Display**: Quicksand Bold (Rounded, friendly, organic)
- **Body**: Quicksand Regular
- **Hierarchy**: Soft, inviting headings. Plenty of whitespace.

## Layout
- **Hero**: Full-bleed macro photograph of cactus skin with water droplets. Overlay text: 'Pure from the Desert' in Dark Grey, centered. CTA: 'Explore Materials' in Cactus-Green, organic pill shape.
- **Navigation**: Transparent top, links in Dark Grey. Hover: Cactus-Green.
- **Sections**:
  1. **Organic Shapes**: Image containers are irregular blob shapes (SVG masks) containing product or source images.
  2. **Sustainable Source**: Text block on left, blob-shaped image of cactus farm on right. Interactive: Hover on image reveals water usage stats in Cactus-Green.
  3. **Product Lifecycle**: Vertical timeline with organic, vine-like connecting lines in Cactus-Green. Icons for each step (harvest, process, create).
  4. **Shop**: Product cards are replaced by simple image links with hover overlay showing price and 'Add to Cart' in Cactus-Green.

## Motion
- **Entrance**: Hero image fades in slowly. Text slides up.
- **Scroll**: Blob shapes have subtle 'breathing' animation (scale 1.0 to 1.02) on scroll.
- **Interaction**: Hover on product images reveals overlay smoothly. Timeline lines draw in on scroll.
- **Water Effect**: Subtle ripple animation on CTA button hover.

## Constraints
- No rigid grids. Use organic, irregular layouts.
- No purple or synthetic colors.
- Images must focus on texture (cactus skin, water).
- Blob shapes must be smooth, not jagged.
- Responsive: Blobs scale, text remains readable. Mobile layout stacks vertically.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Palette uses clay, cactus-green, dark grey.
- Typography is Quicksand (rounded).
- Blob shapes are used for image containers.
- No card components (use image links with overlays).
- Motion includes subtle breathing and drawing animations.
