01 — Sustainable Source

It drinks the rare rain and gives everything back.

The nopal cactus thrives where nothing else can. No irrigation, no pesticides, no ploughing — just sun, soil and a single harvest every eight years. We work with 40 farming cooperatives across the arid highlands.

02 — What the cactus becomes

Three textures, one leaf.

From a single harvest we draw a supple hide, a living pigment and a strong natural fibre. Nothing leaves the plant unused.

Desserto

A soft, breathable hide tanned with the cactus's own tannins. Durable, water-resistant, animal-free.

Leather· 100% plant

Nopal Dye

Deep botanical greens and ochres extracted from leaf and fruit — a colour that fades gently, never harshly.

Pigment· Non-toxic

Agave Weave

Long, resilient fibres spun into cloth and cord — strong enough for a strap, fine enough for a lining.

Fibre· Compostable
03 — Product Lifecycle

From leaf to earth again.

Four honest steps. No hidden chemistry, no landfill, no landfill-bound offcuts.

Step 01

Harvest

Mature leaves are cut by hand every eight years, leaving the plant rooted and thriving. The cut edge seals itself and keeps growing.

Hand cutPlant stays rooted
Step 02

Sun-dry

Leaves are laid on clay terraces and dried by desert sun for three days. No kilns, no fossil heat — only wind and light.

Solar dryingZero kiln energy
Step 03

Press & Tan

Fibre is pressed into sheets and tanned with the cactus's own tannins and a whisper of natural resin. The plant tans its own skin.

Plant-tannedChrome-free
Step 04

Compost

At the end of a long life, the product is returned to soil. It breaks down in months, feeding the very fields the cactus came from.

Fully compostableCircular
