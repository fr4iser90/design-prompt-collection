The Method

Nothing rushed, nothing wasted.

Our starter — a living culture we've kept for nine years — does the slow work. We simply give it time, cool air, and a hot stone.

Baked
fresh
daily
01

Levain

A small culture is fed the night before, left to bloom overnight until it doubles and smells of green apple and yoghurt.

02

Autolyse

Flour and water rest together, hydrating fully so gluten develops gently — no heavy kneading, just patience.

03

Fermentation

Thirty-six hours in a cool room. Wild yeast and bacteria build the open holes and the deep, complex flavour.

04

Shape & Bake

Hand-shaped, proofed in baskets, then launched onto a blazing hearth where steam lifts the crumb and blisters the crust.

Inside the crumb.

Macro studies of the interior — the translucent walls, the open alveoli, the way light passes through a well-fermented loaf.
