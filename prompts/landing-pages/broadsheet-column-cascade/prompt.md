# Broadsheet Column Cascade

Design a landing page for 'The Daily Wire', a modern news aggregator that mimics the layout of a classic broadsheet newspaper.

**Visual Style:**
- Background: Newsprint white (#f5f5f5) with a very subtle dot-matrix texture.
- Color Palette: Black text, red for 'breaking' alerts, and grey for secondary text.
- Typography: Serif for body text (like 'Merriweather' or 'Georgia'), sans-serif for headlines and captions. Small font sizes for dense information.
- Layout: Multi-column layout (3-4 columns on desktop). Justified text alignment. Thin vertical rules separating columns.

**Motion:**
- A horizontal 'ticker tape' at the top or bottom scrolls news headlines continuously.
- Articles 'flow' into the columns as you scroll, like liquid filling a mold.
- Hovering over a headline underlines it with a hand-drawn-style line.

**Constraints:**
- Dense information hierarchy. Use size and weight, not color, to distinguish importance.
- No images dominating the layout; text is the hero.
