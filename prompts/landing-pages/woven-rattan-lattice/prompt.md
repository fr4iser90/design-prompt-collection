# Woven Rattan Lattice

**Concept:** Landing page for 'Loom & Light', artisan rattan furniture. The visual language is the interlacing of natural fibers. Content is 'woven' into the page.

**Art Direction:**
- **Palette:** Natural rattan tan (#C19A6B), darker weave shadow (#8B7355), and bright sunlight cream (#F5F5DC).
- **Texture:** Repeating SVG pattern of woven rattan. Use `clip-path` to reveal text and images.
- **Typography:** Display: 'DM Serif Display' (high contrast, elegant). Body: 'Karla' (geometric sans).
- **Layout:** Sections are defined by 'gaps' in the weave. Large typography is masked by the rattan pattern, so it appears to be behind the material.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Entrance:** The rattan pattern 'weaves' itself across the screen (stroke-dashoffset animation).
  2. **Hover:** Hovering over a product image causes the rattan pattern to 'loosen' (scale up slightly, opacity decrease) to reveal the product clearly.
  3. **Ambient:** Slow parallax shift of the pattern layers to create depth.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/SVG. Use SVG patterns for the weave. CSS blend modes for light/shadow effects.
