# Charcoal Grill Ember Grid

Create a landing page for 'Iron & Ash BBQ'.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Background:** Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a) with subtle noise texture to simulate soot.
- **Hero Element:** A CSS-grid based 'grate' overlay. The grid lines are thin, sharp steel (#333).
- **Interaction:** Hovering over grid cells triggers a radial glow from the center of the cell, revealing a bed of glowing embers (#ff4500) underneath. The glow intensity increases with mouse proximity.
- **Typography:** Bold, sans-serif, all-caps headers in off-white (#f0f0f0). Body text in light grey.
- **Motion:** Smooth transition for ember glow (ease-out). Smoke particles rise slowly from the bottom of the screen, drifting slightly left/right.
- **Layout:** Full-bleed hero. Content sections below have a 'grill-mark' divider (diagonal dark lines).

**Constraints:**
- No rustic wood textures.
- No cartoon flames.
- Embers must look like heat sources, not fire.
