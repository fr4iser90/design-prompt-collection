The Doctrine

Cold steel.
Hot heart.
Nothing wasted.

We cook the way the pit demands — slow, controlled, honest. Every cut passes over a bed of coals we build by hand each dawn, no gas, no shortcuts, no open flame. Just the deep, even glow of a fire that has finished burning and is ready to work.

The Method

How the
fire is built.

Stack & Light

Two hundred pounds of split post oak stacked in a chimney, lit with nothing but newspaper and patience. No lighter fluid touches the pit.

Let it Burn Down

We wait for the flames to die entirely. What's left is a bed of glowing coals — the true heat source, steady and even.

+90 minutes

Set the Grate

Heavy steel bars are laid across the coal bed, banked hot and cold to build cooking zones. The grate becomes the thermostat.
