Pink ⟂ Blue = Purple

Riso has no black ink. Deep purple and near-black are built by overprinting — laying a second fluorescent pass directly on top of the first. The overlap is subtractive, never additive. No gradients exist in this system; every shade is a dot, a halftone, a decision made at the drum.
