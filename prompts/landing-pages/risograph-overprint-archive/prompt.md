# Risograph Overprint Archive

Design a landing page for 'Fluoro Press', an independent risograph print archive. 

**Visual Style:**
- Background: Heavy uncoated paper texture (#f4f1ea) with visible fiber grain.
- Color Palette: Fluorescent Pink (#ff48b0) and Fluorescent Blue (#0077ff). Use `mix-blend-mode: multiply` to simulate ink overprinting where colors overlap (creating deep purple/black).
- Typography: Monospaced or typewriter-style serif for body; bold, blocky sans-serif for headlines.
- Layout: Asymmetric grid, resembling a laid-out zine spread. Elements should feel slightly 'off-register' (1-2px offset) to mimic printing imperfections.

**Motion:**
- Hovering over images reveals the separation of CMYK channels (or just Pink/Blue layers) with a slight jitter.
- Scroll triggers a 'paper shuffle' effect where sections slide in like sheets of paper being fed into a drum.

**Constraints:**
- No digital gradients; all shading must be simulated via halftone dot patterns.
- Text must remain legible despite blend modes.
