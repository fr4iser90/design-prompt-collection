# Solaris Energy Dune

Design a landing page for 'Solaris Energy', a solar power startup. Use a palette of bone-white (#F5F5F5), solar-yellow (#F7C873), and deep charcoal (#1A1A1A). Typography: Modern, tech-focused sans-serif (e.g., Inter, but avoid system default; use custom web font like 'Space Grotesk'). Layout: Flowing, dune-like curves separating sections. Hero: Animated sun rising over dune silhouettes. Data section: Minimalist charts showing energy output. CTA: 'Power the Future'. No purple. Focus on light, heat, and clean tech.
