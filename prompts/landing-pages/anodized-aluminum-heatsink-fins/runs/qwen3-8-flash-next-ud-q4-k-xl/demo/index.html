01 — The Principle

Cold power,
by design.

Every watt of heat that leaves the output stage is a watt that never reaches the air you hear. The Thermal Flow chassis is a heat engine turned inside out — geometry doing the work that fans cannot.

F.01

Passive Flow

Natural convection carries heat up through the fin channels. No impeller, no bearing, no hum — the air itself becomes the cooling loop.

0 RPM
F.02

Heat Dissipation

Each 0.8 mm fin radiates across 2,140 cm² of surface. Thermal energy disperses into the room before it can ever touch the signal path.

61 W MAX
F.03

Thermal Stability

Constant temperature means constant bias. The amplifier never drifts, never compresses, never warms into a different-sounding machine.

±0.4 °C
