# Terra Forma Adobe

Design a landing page for 'Terra Forma', an adobe-brick architectural studio. Use a palette of raw earth tones: sand (#E6DCCF), terracotta clay (#8D7B68), and deep umber (#4A3B32). Typography should be a clean, sans-serif (like Futura or Gotham) to contrast the organic textures. Layout: Asymmetric grid, large hero image of an adobe structure with long, sharp shadows. Sections: 'Material Origin', 'Structural Integrity', 'Project Gallery'. Include subtle heat-haze distortion effects on background elements. No cards. Focus on whitespace and material presence.
