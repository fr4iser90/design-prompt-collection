# Mixology Ice Sphere Geometry — Extended

## Concept
This concept elevates ice from a utility to the star of the show. It treats cocktail making as a molecular science. The design is clean, cool, and geometric. The use of transparency and clarity is key. The layout should feel structured and precise, like a crystal lattice.

## Palette
- **Ice Blue:** #E0F7FA (Light backgrounds, highlights)
- **Cyan:** #00BCD4 (Accents, links, active states)
- **Dark Slate:** #263238 (Text, footer, depth)
- **White:** #FFFFFF (High-contrast text)

## Typography
- **Display:** *Barlow Condensed* or *Oswald*. Bold, tall, impactful.
- **Body:** *Inter* or *Source Sans Pro*. Clean, readable.
- **Rule:** Use uppercase for section headers.

## Layout
- **Hero:** A 3D-rendered ice sphere rotating slowly. Text overlay: "Precision Chill."
- **Menu Section:** A grid of cocktails, each represented by a geometric shape (sphere, cube, cylinder) that rotates on hover.
- **Ice Science Section:** Diagrams showing how different ice shapes affect dilution and temperature.

## Motion Brief
- **Entrance:** Small ice cubes fly in from the edges to form the central sphere.
- **Ambient:** The sphere rotates slowly. Bubbles inside move subtly.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over a cocktail card rotates its geometric icon and changes the background gradient hue.

## Constraints & Acceptance
- No warm, cozy bar vibes. Keep it cool and clinical.
- The ice animations must look realistic, with proper light refraction.
- Mobile experience should simplify the 3D effects but keep the geometric theme.

## Variants
- **Variant A (Light):** White background, blue accents.
- **Variant B (Dark):** Dark slate background, glowing blue accents.
