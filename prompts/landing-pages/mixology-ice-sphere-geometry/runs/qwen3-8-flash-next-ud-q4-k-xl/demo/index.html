01 / The Science of Ice

Geometry is flavour.

Every degree of melt is a degree of taste. Shape dictates surface area, surface area dictates dilution, and dilution dictates the drink. We engineer the lattice so the last sip is as considered as the first.

Fig. 01 — Surface Area : Volume ΔT / Δt · Dilution Curve
  • 01

    Directional Freezing

    Water freezes from one face, pushing dissolved air out of the crystal. What remains is optical glass — a sphere you can read a menu through.

  • 02

    Surface-to-Volume

    The sphere minimises contact area, so it chills without watering. A cube melts ~40% faster than an equal-mass sphere of the same spirit.

  • 03

    Calibrated Geometry

    Cylinders for long sips, shards for aromatic bursts, torus for slow release — each shape is matched to a specific dilution target.
