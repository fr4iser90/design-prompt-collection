# Mixology Ice Sphere Geometry

Create a landing page for 'Solid State', a craft cocktail bar specializing in ice programs.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Palette:** Ice blue (#E0F7FA), cyan (#00BCD4), dark slate (#263238).
- **Layout:** Geometric. Use circles and clear lines. The hero is a perfect sphere of ice.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif with a tech/scientific feel (e.g., Roboto Condensed or similar, but avoid generic Roboto if possible—try 'Barlow' or 'Oswald').
- **Imagery:** Macro shots of ice cracking, bubbles inside ice, clear drinks.

**Motion:**
- **Entrance:** Ice cubes assemble into a sphere.
- **Ambient:** Slow rotation of the ice sphere.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over menu items changes the background color subtly (e.g., gin = blue, whiskey = amber).

**Deliverable:**
- Interactive menu section.
- Focus on the science of ice.
- Clean, cold, crisp aesthetic.
