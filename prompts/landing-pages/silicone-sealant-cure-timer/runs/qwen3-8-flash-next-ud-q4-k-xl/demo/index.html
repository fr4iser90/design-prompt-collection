Material Behaviour

Two states. One continuous bond.

Silicone & Set begins as a thixotropic paste — glossy, self-levelling, forgiving. Over hours it cross-links into a matte elastomer that flexes with the substrate instead of against it.

T+0 · Application

Wet & Glossy

High surface tension keeps the bead proud. Reflective, transparent at the edges, toolable for a clean joint line.

T+24 · Fully Cured

Matte & Flexible

Opaque, soft-touch, non-porous. Elongates to 500% and recovers — absorbing movement, vibration and thermal drift.

Cure Profile · 23°C / 50% RH

A bond that sets on schedule.

Skin forms fast, depth cures steady. The timeline below tracks modulus build-up through to full service load.

T + 12–20 min

Skin Over

Surface loses tack. Bead holds profile; tooling window closes.

T + 4 hours

Handling Strength

~2 mm skin depth. Assembly may be moved without slumping.

T + 24 hours

Full Cure

Complete cross-link. Matte, opaque, fully elastic across the joint.

T + 7 days

Service Load

Peak adhesion and weathering resistance achieved. Ready for duty.
