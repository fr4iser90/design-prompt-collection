# Silicone Sealant Cure

Create a landing page for 'Seal & Set', an industrial adhesive company. The aesthetic is 'soft-machine': clean, calm, precise.

**Visuals:**
- Background: Cool white/light grey gradient.
- Hero: A macro shot of a bead of silicone sealant. 
- Interaction: As the user scrolls or hovers, the bead transitions from a high-gloss, transparent liquid state to a matte, opaque, flexible rubber state.
- Typography: Swiss-style sans-serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk or similar, avoid Inter/Roboto). Thin weights for labels, medium for body.
- Motion: Smooth, viscous morphing animations. No bouncy springs; use easing that feels like curing material.
- Layout: Minimalist, centered content with plenty of whitespace. Technical data displayed in small, monospaced blocks (e.g., viscosity, cure time).

**Constraints:**
- No purple gradients or neon glows.
- Colors limited to whites, cool greys, and a subtle 'rubber' blue-grey accent.
- Focus on the tactile quality of the material: softness, flexibility, matte finish.
