# Forge and Roast Coffee Lab — Extended

Concept: Forge and Roast is a workshop-style coffee lab selling single-origin beans, grinders, and brewing tools. The landing page should feel mechanical and warm: dark matte surfaces, ember accents, and precise product information. The brand name should carry the page, with roast numbers and origin data acting as visual texture rather than decoration.

Palette: #12100f for the matte black base, #f1efe9 for ash-white text and rules, #c8452b for ember-red accents, badges, and primary actions.

Type pairing: Futura for display headlines, Neue Montreal for interface text, and Söhne Mono for roast specs, weights, and origin codes.

Layout desktop: open with a full-bleed hero split between brand typography and a macro product shot. Follow with a roast profile band, a shop module, a craft process strip, and a compact footer.

Layout mobile: stack sections vertically, keep the hero image below the headline, preserve large type, and make the shop grid one column with sticky add-to-cart actions.

Motion brief: entrance: product image reveal with a clipped mask and headline fade; ambient: ember glow pulse on primary button; interaction: hover over a roast label swaps origin data and shifts the accent underline.

Constraints: no cream terracotta cliché, no purple glow, no hero cards, no more than 3 motion types, and no fake urgency or countdown timers.

Acceptance criteria: the page reads as an industrial coffee lab, the palette feels dark and embered, product data is scannable, motion is subtle, and the layout works cleanly from 360px to desktop.

Variants: A uses a single hero product. B uses a three-panel roast grid with the same palette and type.
