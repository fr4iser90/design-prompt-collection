# Track Lane Split Timing

**Concept:** A landing page for 'SplitSecond', a professional sprint timing service. The visual metaphor is the photo-finish camera.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** High-contrast Athletic Red (#FF0000), Pure White (#FFFFFF), and Deep Track Black (#111111). No gradients.
- **Typography:** Heavy, condensed sans-serif (e.g., 'Druk Wide' or 'Anton') for numbers. Monospace for timestamps.
- **Layout:** Horizontal scroll sections that mimic track lanes. Each section is a 'lane' with a runner's silhouette (abstracted) and their data overlay.
- **Motion:** 
  1. **Entrance:** A vertical red 'finish line' sweeps from right to left across the hero.
  2. **Scroll:** As users scroll, horizontal lanes 'pull' into view, simulating the relative motion of a camera tracking a runner.
  3. **Interaction:** Hovering a data point freezes the background 'motion' (blurred lines) and sharpens the text.
