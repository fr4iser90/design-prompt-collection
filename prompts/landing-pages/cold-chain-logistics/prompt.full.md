# Cold Chain Logistics — Extended

**Concept:**
A landing page for 'FrostLine', a logistics company specializing in temperature-controlled transport. The aesthetic is derived from industrial refrigeration units, shipping containers, and cold-storage facilities. It emphasizes reliability, precision, and the critical nature of maintaining specific temperatures.

**Palette:**
- Primary Background: Deep Navy `#0f172a` (Cold, Professional)
- Text/Structure: Frost White `#e2e8f0`
- Accent/Cold: Ice Blue `#38bdf8` (High visibility)
- Alert/Critical: Warning Red `#ef4444`
- Success/Status: Safe Green `#10b981`

**Typography:**
- Headings: Industrial Stencil (e.g., 'Stencil', 'Black Ops One') for a rugged, official look.
- Body/Data: Monospace (e.g., 'Space Mono', 'Roboto Mono') to mimic digital readouts and shipping manifests.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** A rigid, modular grid. Sections are separated by thick, 2px white borders. Each module contains a 'Status Card' with temperature data, location, and ETA.
- **Mobile:** Single column, but the 'Status Card' aesthetic is preserved with rounded corners and heavy borders.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Frost crystals animate onto the screen from the corners, creating a textured overlay that fades slightly to reveal content.
- **Ambient:** Background elements have a subtle 'breathing' effect, simulating cold air movement.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over status cards causes the temperature readout to flash rapidly (simulating a sensor check) before locking onto the final value with a 'lock' sound effect (optional).

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] No soft, rounded 'friendly' shapes. Everything must feel engineered.
- [ ] High contrast for accessibility.
- [ ] Use of stencil fonts must be legible.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The page feels like a control panel for a cold storage facility.
- Motion is subtle and reinforces the 'cold' theme without being distracting.
- Clear hierarchy between critical alerts and informational data.
