# Load Bearing Structure

Create a landing page for 'Load Bearing,' a structural engineering firm. The aesthetic is 'blueprint brought to life': deep blue backgrounds, white line work, and precise annotations.

**Visual Rules:**
- Background: Deep Blueprint Blue (#003366).
- Lines: White (#ffffff) 1px solid lines for grids, borders, and diagrams.
- Accent: Safety Orange (#ff9900) for highlights, key metrics, and CTAs.
- Typography: 'Helvetica Neue' or 'Arial' for body (clean, neutral); 'Consolas' or 'Courier New' for annotations and labels.
- Imagery: Line-art diagrams of structures (beams, trusses, foundations). No photos.
- Grid: Visible underlying grid system. Use dotted lines for secondary guides.

**Motion & Interaction:**
- **Draw-in:** Diagrams animate as if being drawn (stroke-dashoffset).
- **Hover:** Hovering over a diagram highlights specific components in orange and reveals annotations.
- **Scroll:** Sections snap into place like pages of a technical manual.

**Deliverable:** Hero with a large animated beam diagram, plus a 'Projects' section with annotated thumbnails.
