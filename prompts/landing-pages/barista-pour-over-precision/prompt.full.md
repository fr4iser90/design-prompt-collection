# Barista Pour-Over Precision — Extended

## Concept
This concept treats coffee brewing as a precise scientific process. The website should feel like a laboratory or a high-end engineering manual. The focus is on the *flow* of water and the *precision* of the equipment. Avoid any cozy, warm, rustic vibes. Instead, aim for a cool, clinical, yet luxurious aesthetic. The "steam" is not a cloud, but a subtle distortion in the air.

## Palette
- **Charcoal:** #1A1A1A (Background, primary text)
- **White:** #FFFFFF (Accent text, lines, icons)
- **Brass:** #D4AF37 (Interactive elements, highlights, subtle metallic sheen)
- **Water:** Light blue-grey gradients for fluid effects

## Typography
- **Display:** *Futura* or *Montserrat* (Medium/Regular). Clean, geometric, modern.
- **Technical:** *JetBrains Mono* or *Courier Prime* for numbers, units, and labels.
- **Rule:** Use monospace for all data points (e.g., "92°C", "1:16 Ratio").

## Layout
- **Hero:** Full-screen video loop of a perfect pour-over from above. Text overlay is minimal: "The Perfect Pour." 
- **Product Section:** Exploded view diagrams of the dripper, kettle, and filter. Each part is labeled with technical specs.
- **Recipe Section:** A step-by-step guide with animated progress bars that fill as you scroll.

## Motion Brief
- **Entrance:** The hero video fades in. Text appears letter-by-letter with a slight delay.
- **Ambient:** A very subtle parallax effect on the background elements.
- **Interaction:** When hovering over a product part, the diagram highlights that part in brass and displays a tooltip with the material spec (e.g., "Borosilicate Glass").

## Constraints & Acceptance
- No rustic coffee shop imagery (no wood, no plants, no handwritten signs).
- The water animation must be smooth and realistic, not cartoonish.
- Mobile experience must preserve the vertical flow metaphor.

## Variants
- **Variant A (Dark):** Dominant charcoal background, bright white text.
- **Variant B (Light):** Dominant white background, charcoal text, with light grey grid lines.
