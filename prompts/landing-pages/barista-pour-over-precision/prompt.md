# Barista Pour-Over Precision

Create a landing page for 'Vessel & Flow', a premium pour-over coffee equipment brand.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Palette:** Charcoal black (#1A1A1A), stark white (#FFFFFF), brass accent (#D4AF37).
- **Layout:** Centralized, vertical flow. The hero is a live simulation or high-res loop of water hitting coffee grounds.
- **Typography:** Monospace for specs (temperature, weight), clean geometric sans for branding.
- **Imagery:** Extreme close-ups of water droplets, filter paper texture, brass scales.

**Motion:**
- **Entrance:** A line drawing of a dripper draws itself, then fills with water.
- **Ambient:** Subtle ripple effect on the background water surface.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over product specs highlights the corresponding part of the diagram with a brass glow.

**Deliverable:**
- Single-page scroll.
- Interactive spec sheet section.
- No clutter; whitespace is key.
