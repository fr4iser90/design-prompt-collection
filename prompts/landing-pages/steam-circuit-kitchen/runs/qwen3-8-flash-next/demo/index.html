03 — The Flow

How the Steam Works

Four stages, one continuous loop. From ignition to the last drop, heat travels the same path every night.

STAGE 01

Ignite

The core reaches 100° on induction. A single node wakes the whole board.

STAGE 02

Route

Steam channels branch outward through the table, distributing heat to each seat.

STAGE 03

Regulate

Per-seat dials trim the flow. Cool your lane, spike your neighbour's — no interference.

STAGE 04

Reclaim

Condensation is captured, filtered and recirculated. Zero waste, endless steam.
