# Steam Circuit Kitchen — Extended Brief

## Concept
This landing page reimagines the communal experience of steamboat dining through a digital, tech-forward lens. The "steam" is visualized as data or energy flowing through a network (the table), connecting diners. It's vibrant, modern, and energetic, avoiding the traditional red-lantern Chinese restaurant clichés in favor of a sleek, neon-lit aesthetic.

## Palette & Materials
- **Background:** Deep Black (`#0A0A0A`) to simulate the dark of a modern dining room and make the neon pop.
- **Accent:** Electric Red (`#EF4444`) for the "steam" lines, buttons, and highlights. Represents heat and energy.
- **Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for readability.
- **Support:** Dark Grays (`#1F2937`) for card backgrounds and secondary elements.
- **Textures:** Neon glow, matte black surfaces, digital grid lines.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** `Montserrat` or `Poppins` (Bold, Wide) for a modern, impactful look.
- **Body:** `Inter` or `Roboto` for clean, legible body text.
- **Labels:** Uppercase, spaced-out tracking for menu categories.

## Layout & Structure
1. **Hero:** A central, abstract representation of a steam pot. Red neon lines (representing steam) rise and branch out, connecting to floating icons or text elements around the screen. The headline "TUNE YOUR HEAT" is overlaid.
2. **The Menu:** Instead of a traditional list, use a "heat map" or interactive grid. Each dish is represented by a node with a specific color intensity based on spiciness or temperature. Clicking a node expands details.
3. **The Table:** An interactive top-down view of the table layout. Users can toggle "steam paths" to see how heat flows from the center to individual seats. 
4. **Reservation:** A sleek, minimal form. Inputs have a subtle red glow on focus. The CTA button is a bright red rectangle with white text, labeled "INITIATE DINNER."

## Motion Design
- **Ambient:** The neon lines in the hero should gently pulse and flow, mimicking the movement of steam. 
- **Entrance:** Elements fade in with a slight "glitch" effect (brief horizontal shift) to reinforce the digital theme.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over menu nodes causes them to glow brighter and expand slightly. 
- **Scroll:** The background features a very subtle, slow-moving grid pattern that speeds up slightly on scroll, creating a sense of flow.

## Constraints
- Avoid traditional Chinese calligraphy or paper lantern imagery.
- Keep the neon effects sharp and clean, not blurry or hazy.
- Ensure the red accent is used sparingly to maintain contrast and impact.
