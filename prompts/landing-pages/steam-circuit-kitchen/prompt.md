# Steam Circuit Kitchen

Develop a landing page for 'Circuit & Steam', a futuristic steamboat restaurant concept.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Atmosphere:** High-energy, digital, warm. Traditional steam meets cyberpunk circuitry.
- **Palette:** Deep Black (`#0A0A0A`) background, Neon Red (`#EF4444`) for steam paths and accents, White (`#FFFFFF`) for text.
- **Typography:** Futuristic, wide sans-serif (like `Montserrat` or `Poppins`) with clean lines.
- **Hero:** An animation of red neon lines tracing the paths of steam rising from a central pot, forming circuit-like patterns.
- **Sections:** 'The Menu' (items categorized by heat level), 'The Setup' (interactive table layout), 'The Flow' (how the steam works).
- **Motion:** Fluid, rising animations for steam; glowing, pulsing lines for circuits.
