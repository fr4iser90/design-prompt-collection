The making

Fired once.
Never twice.

Vessel No. 01 begins as a single ball of speckled stoneware, centred and pulled on the wheel until the wall reads the light without holding it. The matte ember surface is not a colour — it is a temperature, reached only at the rim of the kiln where the flame passes closest.

Forty vessels share one load. Where each sits decides its tone: deeper umber in the shade, a faint bisque blush where the heat grazes the shoulder. We don't correct that. The firing is the author.

“Material honesty means the mark of the making stays on the thing.”
