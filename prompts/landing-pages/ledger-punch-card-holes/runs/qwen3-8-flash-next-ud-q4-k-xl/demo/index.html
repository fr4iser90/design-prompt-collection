SEC 04 // PIPELINE

How the
Reader Works

Every record is a card. Every card is a decision. PUNCH LOGIC moves data through four deterministic stages — no black boxes, no guesswork, just clean holes in clean order.

VERIFIED · 1964
STEP 01

Ingest

Cards enter the reader at 1200 per minute. Each 80-column record is validated against the schema before it touches the deck.

STEP 02

Decode

The beam scans each column. Punched holes resolve to values; solid holes resolve to nulls. Ambiguity is rejected, never guessed.

STEP 03

Process

Batch or streaming — your call. Operators run across the deck in parallel, each hole a unit of work on the throughput engine.

STEP 04

Verify

Output cards are re-read and checksummed. A single mismatched hole triggers a red stamp and a full re-run of the batch.

SEC 07 // FAQ
▸ Do I need real cards?
No. The card is a metaphor and a control surface. Configure your deck on screen; we handle the bytes behind it.
▸ What happens on an error?
A red stamp marks the batch, the offending hole is logged, and the deck is re-run automatically. No silent failures.
▸ Can I mix batch and streaming?
Yes. Select the modules you need in the console above; the engine routes each record to the right pipeline.
▸ How is pricing calculated?
Per punched module, per month. The console totals your selection live before you commit the cards.
