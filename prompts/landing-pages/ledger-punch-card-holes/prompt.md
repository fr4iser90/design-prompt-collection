# Ledger Punch Card Holes

Design a landing page for 'Punch Logic', a high-throughput data processing engine. The aesthetic is 'retro-mainframe': the visual language of IBM punch cards from the 1960s, updated for modern clarity.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** 'Card Stock' beige (#F5F5DC).
- **Primary Visual:** A large, stylized punch card layout. Instead of data, the 'holes' are interactive elements. Some holes are 'punched' (dark, indicating selected data), some are 'solid' (light, indicating unselected).
- **Typography:** 'Courier New' or 'IBM Plex Mono' for all text. No serifs, no sans-serif.
- **Accents:** A red 'error' stamp or a red 'priority' hole. Thin, straight lines for borders.
- **Layout:** The page is structured like a stack of cards. Sections are separated by perforated lines (dotted lines).

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS implementation with a grid of interactive 'punch holes' as navigation or content selectors.
