# Microfilm Splice Reel

Design a landing page for 'Splice Studio', a service that migrates legacy microfilm and paper records to digital archives. The aesthetic is 'industrial archive': high-contrast, mechanical, and precise.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Color Palette:** Deep black background (#111111), off-white text (#EEEEEE), and a single accent color: 'Archival Green' (#CCFF00) for interactive states and key data points.
- **Hero Element:** A horizontal, scrolling filmstrip visualization. The 'frames' are not photos, but abstract representations of data (barcodes, QR codes, dense text blocks). A 'splice' tape effect connects segments of the filmstrip.
- **Typography:** Monospaced font for all data labels (e.g., 'IBM Plex Mono' or 'Roboto Mono') to suggest machine-readability. A bold, condensed sans-serif for headlines (e.g., 'Oswald' or 'Bebas Neue') to suggest industrial signage.
- **Layout:** Full-width sections. The filmstrip moves horizontally as the user scrolls vertically, creating a sense of 'reading' the archive.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS implementation with a scroll-driven horizontal animation for the filmstrip.
