[ 02 ] — PIPELINE

The Splice Line

Five stations. Each reel travels the same path, from physical intake to a permanent digital record. Hover a node to inspect.

ST-01

Intake

STATION 01 · INTAKEReels logged, condition-graded, and assigned a barcode. Chain-of-custody begins here.
ST-02

Inspection

STATION 02 · INSPECTIONEmulsion checked for silver-rot, scratches, and breaks. Splice points mapped before scanning.
ST-03

Scan

STATION 03 · SCANFrame-accurate capture at archival resolution. Every frame verified against the physical reel.
ST-04

Index

STATION 04 · INDEXOCR extraction, barcode decode, and metadata tagging. Frames become searchable records.
ST-05

Deliver

STATION 05 · DELIVERChecksummed archive handed over with API access and full audit trail. Originals returned or stored.
