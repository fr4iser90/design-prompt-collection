# Microfilm Splice Reel — Extended

**Concept:**
'Splice Studio' takes messy, physical archives and turns them into clean, searchable digital databases. The metaphor is the physical act of splicing film—taking disparate pieces and joining them into a coherent whole. The visual language should evoke the machinery of a microfilm reader: dark, focused, with a greenish tint from the screen, and the tactile feel of plastic tape.

**Palette:**
- `#111111` (Archive Dark): Background.
- `#EEEEEE` (Film Base): Primary text.
- `#CCFF00` (Cathode Green): Accent, links, active states, 'success' indicators.
- `#333333` (Machine Grey): Borders, inactive elements.

**Typography:**
- **Headlines:** 'Oswald' (Bold). All-caps. Tight tracking. Large size.
- **Body/Labels:** 'IBM Plex Mono'. Small to medium size. High contrast against the dark background.
- **Filmstrip Text:** Tiny, simulated text within the 'frames' (can be actual small text or blurred lines) to suggest density.

**Layout:**
- **Hero:** A full-viewport section. The background is a subtle grid of dark squares (like a light table). A large, bold headline "MIGRATE YOUR HISTORY" sits center-left. A horizontal filmstrip element runs across the bottom 30% of the screen. It moves slowly on its own, but speeds up/changes direction based on scroll.
- **Services:** Three columns, but styled as 'technical specs'. Each column has a header in Oswald, a description in Plex Mono, and a 'status' light (a small green dot).
- **Process:** A timeline that looks like a circuit board or a splice line. Dots are connected by thin lines. Hovering over a step highlights it in green and expands a tooltip with more info.
- **Footer:** A simple black bar. White text. No social media. Just 'Contact', 'Privacy', 'Terms'.

**Motion:**
- **Scroll-Driven Filmstrip:** As the user scrolls down, the filmstrip moves left. When it hits a 'splice' (a visual break in the strip), it pauses briefly, then snaps to the next segment. This simulates the mechanical action of a reader.
- **Hover Effects:** Hovering over links underlines them with a 'tape' effect (a green rectangle appearing). Hovering over filmstrip frames zooms them in slightly and adds a 'scanline' overlay.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] No rounded corners. Everything is sharp, rectangular, or circular (reels).
- [ ] No gradients except for subtle vignettes on the filmstrip.
- [ ] The green accent must be used sparingly—only for interaction or key data.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
The page should feel like interacting with a piece of high-end archival equipment. It is serious, efficient, and slightly retro-futuristic.
