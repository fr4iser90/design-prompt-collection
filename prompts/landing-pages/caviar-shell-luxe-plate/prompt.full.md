# Caviar Shell Luxe Plate — Extended

## Concept
This concept embodies the ultimate luxury: caviar. The design should feel exclusive, quiet, and refined. It’s not about shouting "buy now," but whispering "experience perfection." The visual language is drawn from high-jewelry advertisements and fine-art photography. The black background represents the void from which the luxury emerges. The gold accents are restrained, not gaudy.

## Palette
- **Void Black:** #000000 (Background)
- **Charcoal:** #111111 (Subtle depth, overlays)
- **Antique Gold:** #C5A059 (Accents, borders, key text)
- **Pearl White:** #F0F0F0 (Small text details)

## Typography
- **Display:** *Bodoni Moda* or *Didot*. Extremely high contrast between thick and thin strokes.
- **Body:** *Raleway* (Light 300). Very small size, wide letter-spacing for elegance.
- **Rule:** Never bold. Use weight variations for emphasis.

## Layout
- **Hero:** A single, perfect caviar pearl centered on a black background. Title: "Aureum." Subtitle: "Black Gold."
- **Product Grid:** Minimal cards with large images of tins. No borders. Just image and name.
- **Story Section:** A timeline of the sturgeon's life, illustrated with delicate line art.

## Motion Brief
- **Entrance:** The hero pearl drops slowly from the top, settling with a gentle bounce (spring physics).
- **Ambient:** A very slow, radial gradient shift in the background to simulate depth.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over a product card lifts it slightly (translateY -5px) and adds a soft gold shadow.

## Constraints & Acceptance
- No bright colors or playful elements.
- Images must be high-resolution and sharply focused.
- The loading experience should be seamless and fast, despite high-res assets.

## Variants
- **Variant A (Classic):** Black background, gold text.
- **Variant B (Inverted):** White background, black text, with subtle pearl texture.
