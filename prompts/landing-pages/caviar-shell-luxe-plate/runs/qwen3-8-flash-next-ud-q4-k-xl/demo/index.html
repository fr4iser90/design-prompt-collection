The Ritual

From silence
to the spoon.

Four movements, unchanged since the house was founded. No machine touches the pearl.

I

The Catch

Drawn at first light from the deep, cold current of the Caspian.

II

The Sort

Each pearl weighed by eye, graded on firmness, sheen and salt.

III

The Cure

A whisper of salt — malossol — never more, never less.

IV

The Spoon

Served on mother-of-pearl, so the metal never touches the gold.

It is everything you have the restraint to leave out.”
