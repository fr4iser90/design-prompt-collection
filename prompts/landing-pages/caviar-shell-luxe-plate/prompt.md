# Caviar Shell Luxe Plate

Create a landing page for 'Aureum Caviar', a high-end caviar producer.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Palette:** Deep black (#000000), charcoal (#111111), antique gold (#C5A059).
- **Layout:** Minimalist, spacious. Large, isolated product shots. Negative space is paramount.
- **Typography:** Elegant serif (e.g., Didot or Bodoni) for headlines, light sans-serif for body.
- **Imagery:** Macro shots of individual caviar pearls, mother-of-pearl spoons, dark stone plates.

**Motion:**
- **Entrance:** Pearls gently float into place like a slow-motion drop.
- **Ambient:** A subtle shimmer effect on the gold accents.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over a product reveals its origin and tasting notes in a floating, glass-like panel.

**Deliverable:**
- Premium, slow-paced feel.
- No aggressive CTAs; use subtle links.
- Focus on craftsmanship and rarity.
