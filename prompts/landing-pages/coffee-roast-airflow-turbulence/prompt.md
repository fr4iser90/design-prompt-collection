# Coffee Roast Airflow Turbulence

Design a landing page for 'Turbine & Bean', a commercial coffee roasting technology company.

**Visual Concept:**
The hero is not a bean, but the *air* around the bean. A dynamic vector field visualization shows hot air flowing through a drum. The color gradient maps temperature: cool greys (#2C2C22) to intense, glowing orange-red (#FF5733).

**Key Elements:**
1.  **Hero Animation:** A canvas-based particle system representing airflow. Particles speed up and change color (grey to orange) as they pass through the 'roast zone' (center screen). The flow reacts to mouse proximity by dispersing.
2.  **Data Overlay:** Thin, white lines overlay the airflow showing real-time temperature curves (e.g., '195°C', '205°C'). These lines are crisp and technical.
3.  **Typography:** Industrial sans-serif (e.g., 'Archivo' or 'Oswald'). Bold, uppercase headings that feel heavy and mechanical.
4.  **Color Palette:** Charcoal (#111111), Dark Grey (#2C2C2C), and Ember Orange (#FF5733). No browns or creams.

**Constraints:**
- No rustic coffee shop vibes (no burlap, no script fonts).
- The focus is on *engineering* and *control*, not 'cozy'.
- Use of neon/orange must be subtle, not glowing/hazy, but sharp and vector-like.
