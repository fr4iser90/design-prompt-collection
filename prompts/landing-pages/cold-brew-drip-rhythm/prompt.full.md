# Cold Brew Drip Rhythm — Extended

## Concept
Cold brew is about patience and precision. This design reflects that through extreme minimalism and slow, deliberate motion. The core metaphor is the 'drip' as a time marker. Each drop represents a unit of time, building up to the final product. The aesthetic is 'cold' and 'clean'—no warmth, no steam, just the stark beauty of the process.

## Palette
- **Base:** Deep Slate (#0f172a) for the background, evoking a dark, cold room.
- **Secondary:** Slate Grey (#334155) for UI elements and secondary text.
- **Accent:** Off-White (#e2e8f0) for text and the 'drop' highlight.
- **No Warm Colors:** Avoid browns, oranges, or creams. This is 'cold' brew.

## Typography
- **Display:** A very light, wide sans-serif (e.g., 'Helvetica Neue Light' or 'Arial Light') to convey spaciousness and calm.
- **Body:** Small, tight sans-serif for technical details (brew time, ratio).

## Layout
- **Hero:** Centered. The dripper SVG is at the top 1/3. The 'pool' is at the bottom 1/3. The middle is empty space (negative space).
- **Content:** As the user scrolls, sections 'drip' into view. Each section is separated by a subtle horizontal line (like a water level rising).
- **Footer:** Minimal, with a single 'Buy' button that is understated.

## Motion Brief
1. **Entrance:** The dripper fades in. The first drop falls after 1 second.
2. **Ambient:** The drop falls every 2 seconds. The ripple expands slowly (3-second duration) and fades out.
3. **Interaction:** 
   - **Scroll:** Scroll-driven animations. Sections slide up slowly as they enter the viewport.
   - **Hover:** Hovering over the 'pool' area intensifies the ripple effect.

## Constraints
- **No Steam:** Cold brew has no steam. Do not use any steam animations.
- **No Brown:** The coffee itself is not visible. Only the process is visualized via light and water.
- **Slow Motion:** All animations must be slow. No bouncing or snappy effects.
- **Minimalism:** Keep UI elements to a bare minimum.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The drop timing must feel rhythmic and calming.
- The color palette must be strictly cool-toned.
- The layout must use significant negative space.
- The 'drip' metaphor must be clear and not literal (no cartoon drops).
