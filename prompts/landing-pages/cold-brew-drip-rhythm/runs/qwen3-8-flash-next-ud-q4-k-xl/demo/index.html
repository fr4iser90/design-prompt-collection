01 — The Method

/ 01

Grind

Coarse. Uneven is honest. The grind sets the pace of every drop that follows.

/ 02

Settle

Bed the grounds flat, then let them rest. Water finds its own path through patience.

/ 03

Percolate

One drop every two seconds. The vessel fills slowly, deliberately, without a single degree of heat.

/ 04

Decant

When the level holds steady, the concentrate is done. Pour it over ice — nothing else needed.
