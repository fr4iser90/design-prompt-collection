# Cold Brew Drip Rhythm

Create a landing page for 'Slow Drip Coffee'.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Background:** Deep Slate (#0f172a) to represent the cold, dark brewing vessel.
- **Hero Element:** A minimalist SVG animation of a coffee dripper. A single drop falls every 2 seconds into a pool.
- **Interaction:** When a drop hits the 'surface' (a circular area), it creates a ripple effect. The ripple expands and fades. Text content is revealed via these ripples (e.g., 'Steep for 12 Hours' appears as a ripple).
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif, light weight. White (#e2e8f0) text.
- **Layout:** Vertical flow. The dripper is top-center. Content cascades down as if 'dripped' into place.
- **Motion:** Slow, deliberate timing. No fast animations. Easing should be cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1).

**Constraints:**
- No steaming cups.
- No brown 'coffee' colors—stick to dark slate/grey/black.
- No busy backgrounds.
