# Chime Resonance Brand

Create a landing page for 'Chime', a sonic branding agency. The hero features a massive, sans-serif wordmark 'CHIME' that subtly vibrates based on user scroll speed or mouse proximity. Letters should act as resonant chambers, with thin vertical lines (sound waves) emanating from the center of each glyph. Use a stark black and white palette with a single accent color (#e63946) for active states. Typography must be expressive, large, and tightly kerned. Avoid static layouts; motion must feel physical and auditory.
