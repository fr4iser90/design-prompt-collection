# Kettle & Kiln — Extended Brief

**Concept:**
'Kettle & Kiln' sells hand-thrown porcelain tea sets that utilize vacuum siphon brewing for clarity and purity. The website should feel calm, ritualistic, and precise. The core interaction is the brewing process itself, which becomes a meditative animation.

**Palette:**
- **Background:** Warm Bone (#f9f7f5) — not pure white, to feel organic.
- **Porcelain:** Matte White (#ffffff) with soft gray shadows (#e0d8d0).
- **Metal:** Brushed Steel (#c0c0c0 to #999999 gradient).
- **Tea/Water:** Clear (#e6f0ff) transitioning to Amber (#8b3a3a) as it 'steeps'.
- **Accent:** Deep Charcoal (#5c4d42) for text.

**Type Pairing:**
- **Display:** 'Cormorant Garamond' or 'Playfair Display' (Light weight). Evokes tradition and elegance.
- **Body:** 'Lato' or 'Open Sans' (Regular). Clean and readable.

**Layout Desktop:**
1. **Hero:** The siphon apparatus is centered, taking up 70% of vertical space. 
2. **Left Panel:** Brand story. 'The Art of Clarity'. Short paragraph.
3. **Right Panel:** Technical specs. '12-piece Set', 'Hand-glazed', 'Thermal Shock Resistant'.
4. **Bottom:** A 'Brew' button (minimal, text-only with an arrow icon).

**Layout Mobile:**
1. **Hero:** Siphon takes up top 50%. 
2. **Content:** Stacked below. The 'Brew' button is fixed at the bottom or prominent in the flow.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Elements fade in slowly (1.5s). The siphon 'assembles' itself (parts sliding into place).
- **Ambient:** Steam wisps drift slowly upward from the top valve, looping infinitely but subtly.
- **Interaction (Click 'Brew'):** 
  1. Lower chamber fills with water (blue-grey).
  2. Bubbles form in the lower chamber.
  3. Water travels up the tube (animated stroke).
  4. Upper chamber fills, changing from transparent to opaque porcelain white.
  5. Color shift to amber.
  6. 'Steep' text appears briefly, then fades.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] No heavy drop shadows on the object; use soft ambient shadows on the floor.
- [ ] Animation must be smooth (60fps). Use SVG animations or Canvas.
- [ ] Colors must remain muted and earthy. No bright primaries.
- [ ] Ensure contrast between text and background is sufficient for readability.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
The user feels a sense of calm and anticipation when clicking 'Brew'. The transition from empty to full to 'steeped' is satisfying and clear. The brand feels artisanal yet modern.
