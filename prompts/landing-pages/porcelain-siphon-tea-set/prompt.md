# Kettle & Kiln: Interactive Porcelain Siphon

Design a landing page for 'Kettle & Kiln', a high-end ceramic tea set.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Hero Element:** A central, stylized SVG or 3D render of a vacuum siphon coffee/tea maker. 
- **Materiality:** The lower chamber is brushed stainless steel (#e0d8d0). The upper chamber is translucent porcelain (#f9f7f5) with a slight warm undertone.
- **Interaction:** 
  1. Default state: Empty chambers.
  2. Hover: A subtle steam wisp rises from the top valve.
  3. Click 'Brew': Water fills the lower chamber (blue-grey), then animates upward through a glass tube into the upper chamber. As it fills, the upper chamber's opacity increases, and the water color shifts to amber (#8b3a3a).
- **Layout:** Centered composition. Text is placed to the left and right, balancing the object. 
- **Typography:** Elegant serif for brand name ('Kettle & Kiln'), clean sans-serif for body.
- **Background:** Warm off-white (#f9f7f5) with a very subtle radial gradient to simulate studio lighting.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS implementation of the siphon animation.
