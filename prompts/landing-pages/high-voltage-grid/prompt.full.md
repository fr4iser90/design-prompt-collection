# High Voltage Grid — Extended

**Concept:**
A landing page for 'VoltEdge', a software platform for managing high-voltage energy grids. The design borrows heavily from industrial safety signage and electrical utility aesthetics. It conveys power, danger, and precision control.

**Palette:**
- Primary Background: Matte Black `#1a1a1a`
- Hazard: Safety Yellow `#ffd700` (High visibility)
- Text: Pure White `#ffffff`
- Alert: Electric Orange `#ff4500`
- Accent: Deep Black `#000000` (for contrast with yellow)

**Typography:**
- Headings: Bold, geometric sans-serif (e.g., 'Inter', 'Helvetica Now') for a modern, technical feel.
- Data: Monospace (e.g., 'Fira Code') for voltage readings and system status.

**Layout:**
- **Desktop:** Large, impactful typography. Sections are divided by diagonal hazard stripes. Content is aligned to a sharp, left-heavy grid.
- **Mobile:** Stacked layout, but hazard stripes remain prominent as dividers.

**Motion Brief:**
- **Entrance:** Headlines slide in from the left with a sharp, 'cut' transition.
- **Ambient:** Subtle electrical arcs (thin, white/yellow lines) flicker in the background, simulating energy flow.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over interactive elements triggers a small 'spark' particle effect. Clicking buttons causes a brief 'power surge' flash on the screen border.

**Constraints Checklist:**
- [ ] Avoid clutter. The 'danger' aesthetic should be clean, not messy.
- [ ] Ensure text is always readable against hazard stripes (use solid overlays if needed).
- [ ] No organic shapes. Everything is angular and precise.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The page feels powerful and slightly intimidating, yet professional.
- Hazard stripes are used purposefully, not just as decoration.
- Motion reinforces the theme of electrical energy.
