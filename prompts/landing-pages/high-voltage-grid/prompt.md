# High Voltage Grid

Design a landing page for 'VoltEdge', a grid management software.

**Visuals:**
- **Background:** Matte black `#1a1a1a` with subtle, animated electrical arcs (thin, jagged lines) that flicker occasionally.
- **Typography:** Bold, sans-serif headings (e.g., 'Inter', 'Helvetica Now') for clarity, and monospace for code/data.
- **Elements:** Use diagonal hazard stripes (yellow `#ffd700` and black `#000000`) as section dividers and button backgrounds. Include 'DANGER: HIGH VOLTAGE' style icons.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric layout with large blocks of text interrupted by sharp, angular graphic elements.

**Motion:**
- Electrical arcs pulse faintly in the background.
- Buttons have a 'spark' effect on hover (small, quick particle burst).
- Text sections slide in sharply from the left with a 'cut' effect.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS.
