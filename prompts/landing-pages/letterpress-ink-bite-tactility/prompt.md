# Letterpress Ink Bite Tactility

Design a landing page for 'Press & Bite', a traditional letterpress studio.

**Visual Style:**
- Background: Heavy, warm cotton paper texture (#e0dcd3).
- Color Palette: Dark ink grey (#333333), deep crimson (#8b0000), and paper white.
- Typography: High-contrast serif fonts (e.g., 'Playfair Display' or 'Bodoni') with strong weight variations.
- Effect: Text and elements should appear 'pressed' into the paper. Use `box-shadow` with inset dark shadows and outer light highlights to simulate depth.

**Motion:**
- On hover, text or buttons 'sink' deeper into the paper (increase inset shadow).
- Images reveal with a 'plate' effect, as if the metal type plate is being lifted to reveal the ink.
- Scroll parallax on the paper texture itself, making the grain feel tangible.

**Constraints:**
- No flat design. Everything must have perceived physical depth.
- Ink texture should look slightly imperfect, with some 'bite' into the paper fibers.
