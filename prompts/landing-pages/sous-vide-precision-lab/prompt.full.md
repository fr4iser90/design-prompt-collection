# Sous-Vide Precision Lab — Extended Brief

## Concept
This landing page embodies the philosophy of sous-vide cooking: control, precision, and science. It rejects the warm, rustic clichés of food websites in favor of a sterile, high-performance aesthetic. The user should feel they are configuring a scientific instrument, not just buying a kitchen gadget.

## Palette & Materials
- **Background:** Deep Slate Blue (`#0F172A`) to evoke the depth of water and the dark of a professional kitchen.
- **Accent:** Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) used strictly for data visualization, active states, and temperature indicators.
- **Neutral:** Cool White (`#F8FAFC`) and Slate Gray (`#94A3B8`) for typography and secondary borders.
- **Textures:** Glass reflections, water refractions, and plastic-like smoothness of vacuum bags.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** `Inter` or `Helvetica Neue` (tight tracking, uppercase) for authoritative statements.
- **Data/UI:** `Roboto Mono` or `IBM Plex Mono` for all numbers, labels, and technical specs. This creates a clear hierarchy between brand voice and technical data.

## Layout & Structure
1. **Hero:** Full-viewport. Centered product image (the circulator) surrounded by floating, animated UI cards showing temperature gradients. The background has a very subtle, slow-moving caustic light effect.
2. **Precision Section:** A split layout. Left: Large, clear typography about "±0.1°C Accuracy." Right: An animated line graph showing temperature stability over time.
3. **The Seal:** A section focusing on the vacuum bag. Use images that highlight the clarity of the plastic and the freshness of the food inside. Overlay text: "Oxygen is the enemy."
4. **Recipe Matrix:** A grid of "perfectly cooked" results. Instead of appetizing photos, use close-ups of texture (crust, grain, moisture) with data labels (Time, Temp, Outcome).

## Motion Design
- **Entrance:** Elements fade in with a slight upward drift, mimicking bubbles rising. 
- **Ambient:** The temperature readout in the hero should gently pulse or update to show live changes.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over product features highlights specific technical specs in cyan.
- **Scroll:** Parallax effect on the background caustics to create depth, but keep it subtle (10-15% speed difference).

## Constraints
- No rustic wood textures.
- No warm yellows or oranges.
- Maintain a consistent 8pt grid.
- Ensure all text has high contrast against the dark background.
