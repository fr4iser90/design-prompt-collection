01 / Philosophy

Made to be felt

Every surface is designed for the hand before the eye. Weight, temperature, and the sound of a latch closing are treated as first-class features.

A drawer should announce itself with one clean, weighted click.

We obsess over the millimetre of travel between your fingers and the cabinet face. The resistance you feel on the rail above is not a trick — it is a translation of the same spring-loaded detent machined into every hinge we ship.

Knobs are turned on a lathe, hand-polished, and finished in small batches. The result is hardware that feels inevitable under the hand.
