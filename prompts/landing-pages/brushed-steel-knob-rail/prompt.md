# Rail & Knob: Draggable Hardware Hero

Create a landing page for 'Rail & Knob' cabinet hardware.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Hero Interaction:** A horizontal 'rail' across the center. On the rail sit 3-5 large, brushed steel knobs.
- **Drag Mechanic:** Users can drag the knobs left/right along the rail. 
- **Magnetic Snap:** As you drag, there is visual 'resistance' (slight lag). When you release near a 'stop' position, the knob snaps to it with a slight bounce (elastic easing).
- **Content Reveal:** Each 'stop' corresponds to a product model (e.g., 'Model 01: Matte', 'Model 02: Polished'). Dragging a knob to a stop updates the text content below the rail.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast. Dark background (#111111). Knobs are bright, reflective steel (#cccccc with specular highlights). Rail is dark matte (#333333).
- **Typography:** Bold, condensed sans-serif for model names. Light, wide for descriptions.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS with drag-and-drop logic and magnetic snap effect.
