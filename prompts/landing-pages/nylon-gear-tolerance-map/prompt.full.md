# Nylon Gear Tolerance — Extended Brief

## Concept
'Polymer & Pitch' makes nylon gears that are quiet, durable, and precise. The website should communicate these qualities through a clean, technical, and calm design. The hero visual is a macro shot of gears with a tolerance map, highlighting the precision of their manufacturing.

## Palette
- **Background:** `#F5F5F5` (Light Grey)
- **Gear Material:** `#E0E0E0` to `#D1D1D1` (Matte White/Grey)
- **Tolerance Good:** `#A3B18A` (Muted Green)
- **Tolerance Caution:** `#E0C9A6` (Muted Yellow/Beige)
- **Text:** `#333333` (Dark Grey)
- **Grid/Lines:** `#888888` (Medium Grey)

## Typography
- **Headings:** 'DM Sans' or 'Work Sans'. Clean, geometric sans-serif.
- **Body:** 'DM Sans' or 'Work Sans' Light.
- **Technical/Labels:** 'Space Mono' or 'JetBrains Mono'. Monospaced, small, uppercase.

## Layout
- **Hero:** Centered visual of two interlocking gears. The background has a subtle dot grid. Text is placed to the side, allowing the gears to be the focus.
- **Features Section:** 3-column grid. Each feature (e.g., 'Low Noise', 'High Durability') has an icon and short description. Icons are line-art style.
- **Contact Section:** Simple form with clean inputs and a button that matches the gear material color.

## Motion Design
- **Ambient:** The gears rotate slowly (e.g., 1 rotation per 10 seconds). The motion is linear and smooth.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over the gears causes them to stop rotating and zoom in slightly. The tolerance map overlay fades in, showing color-coded zones on the gear teeth. The cursor changes to a 'zoom-in' icon.
- **Scroll:** Elements fade in and slide up slightly as they enter the viewport. The gears continue to rotate in the background if visible.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] No Inter, Roboto, Arial.
- [ ] No shiny metallic effects. Use matte finishes.
- [ ] No neon colors. Use muted, earthy tones for the tolerance map.
- [ ] Ensure the site feels 'quiet' and 'smooth'.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The gear rotation is smooth and continuous.
- The tolerance map is clear and informative.
- The overall design is clean, technical, and calm.
