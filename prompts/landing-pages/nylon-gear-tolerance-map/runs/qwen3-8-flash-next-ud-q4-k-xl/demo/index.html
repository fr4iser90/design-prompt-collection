Capabilities

Engineered for the quietest machines.

Every gear is designed, moulded and measured in-house — so smoothness is a specification, not a hope.

01 / Acoustics

Low-noise profile

Optimised tooth geometry and a self-lubricating nylon matrix cut meshing noise to under 42 dB — quieter than a library.

Sound pressure · <42 dB(A)
02 / Longevity

High durability

Glass-filled PA66 resists fatigue, creep and wear across 10⁸ cycles — holding its tolerance long after metal gears have worn.

Rated life · 10⁸ cycles
03 / Precision

±0.02 mm tolerance

In-mould cooling and post-process metrology keep every tooth flank, root and pitch circle inside a 20-micron window.

Measured · CMM, 100%
04 / Friction

Self-lubricating

Nylon runs dry and clean — no grease, no contamination, no maintenance windows. Ideal for food, medical and optics.

Lubricant · 0 ml

Corrosion-proof

Chemically inert and immune to rust, our gears thrive in humid, saline and washdown environments where steel simply fails.

IP-rated · washdown safe

Lightweight

At roughly one-sixth the density of steel, polymer gearing slashes rotating mass — improving acceleration and efficiency.

Mass · ~⅙ of steel
