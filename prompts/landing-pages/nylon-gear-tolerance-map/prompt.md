# Nylon Gear Tolerance

Design a landing page for 'Polymer & Pitch', a manufacturer of precision nylon gears. The aesthetic is 'Soft-Machine': clean, technical, and quiet.

**Visuals:**
- Background: Light grey (`#F5F5F5`) with a subtle dot grid.
- Hero: A macro view of interlocking nylon gears. The gears are rendered in a matte, off-white material.
- Overlay: A 'tolerance map' overlay that appears on hover. It uses color-coded zones (green for good, yellow for caution) to show manufacturing precision. The colors are muted, not neon.
- Typography: 'Roboto Mono' is forbidden. Use 'JetBrains Mono' or 'Space Mono' for technical labels. 'Inter' is forbidden. Use 'Work Sans' or 'DM Sans' for headings.
- Motion: Gears rotate slowly and smoothly. The rotation speed is constant, suggesting precision. Hovering over a gear stops it momentarily and zooms in.

**Constraints:**
- No metallic shiny surfaces. The gears are plastic/nylon.
- No loud industrial colors (red/yellow hazard). Use muted greens and greys.
- No Inter, Roboto, Arial.
- Focus on 'quiet operation' and 'smoothness'.
