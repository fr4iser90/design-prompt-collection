# Archer Scope Stabilization

**Concept:** Landing page for 'SteadyHand', a high-end archery equipment brand. The UX mimics the act of aiming: initial uncertainty (blur) resolving into perfect clarity (sharpness) through user action.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Matrix Green (#00FF41) for crosshairs/UI, Deep Black (#050505) background, Dark Grey (#333333) for inactive elements.
- **Typography:** Minimalist, geometric sans-serif (e.g., 'Space Grotesk'). Thin weights for elegance, bold for data.
- **Layout:** Centered, circular 'scope' view in the hero. Content is revealed within the circular mask.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Idle:** Background image is heavily blurred. A subtle 'breathing' sine wave motion shakes the scope slightly.
  2. **Action:** User clicks and holds (or scrolls down). The blur reduces to 0, the shake stops, and the crosshair locks onto the 'Buy' button.
  3. **Release:** Blur returns slowly.
