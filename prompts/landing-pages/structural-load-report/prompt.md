# Structural Load Report

Design a landing page for 'LoadSafe', a structural monitoring service.

**Visuals:**
- **Background:** Dark blue `#0a192f` with a faint, white blueprint grid pattern.
- **Typography:** Clean, technical sans-serif (e.g., 'IBM Plex Sans') for body, and monospace for data.
- **Elements:** 'APPROVED' and 'INSPECTION REQUIRED' stamps. Load bar charts with warning zones. Technical annotations with arrows pointing to key stats.
- **Layout:** A report-style layout with headers, footers, and section numbers. Heavy use of borders and dividers.

**Motion:**
- Blueprint grid lines draw themselves in on load.
- 'Stamp' effects appear with a 'thud' animation on scroll.
- Load bars fill up dynamically, turning from green to yellow to red as they exceed thresholds.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS.
