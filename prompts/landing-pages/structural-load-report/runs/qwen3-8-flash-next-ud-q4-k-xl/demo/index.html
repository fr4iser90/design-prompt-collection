§04MONITORING METHODPIPELINE · v4

From sensor to sealed report

  1. Instrument

    Strain gauges, accelerometers, load cells and fiber-optic arrays are bonded to load-bearing members at design-critical nodes.

  2. Acquire

    Edge gateways sample at up to 1 kHz, timestamping every reading to a tamper-evident ledger.

  3. Validate

    Readings are checked against certified capacity envelopes and flagged against 70% / 85% thresholds.

  4. Certify

    Compliant structures receive a digital seal; exceptions route to a licensed engineer for disposition.
