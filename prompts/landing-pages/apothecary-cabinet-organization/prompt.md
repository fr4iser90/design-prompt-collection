# Apothecary Cabinet Organization

**Concept:** A landing page for a digital archive tool that prioritizes order and retrieval. Visual metaphor: The browser window is a physical cabinet. Sections are drawers.

**Visual Rules:**
1. **North Light Aesthetic:** High-key, diffused lighting. Shadows are soft but directional (top-left). No dark mode.
2. **Materiality:** Matte paper textures, thin brass handles, glass fronts. No heavy skeuomorphism, just hints of material.
3. **Typography:** High-contrast Serif for headings (e.g., Playfair Display or similar), crisp Monospace for metadata.

**Motion Logic:**
- **Drawer Slide:** Clicking a nav item "opens" a drawer from the bottom up. Content inside slides in with a slight delay (staggered).
- **Indexing:** Hovering over a "card" lifts it slightly and reveals a back-side annotation.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS landing page focusing on the drawer interaction.
