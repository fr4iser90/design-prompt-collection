# Noodle Hydrocolloid Shear

Design a landing page for 'Gluten & Gauge', a commercial pasta equipment brand.

**Visual Concept:**
The hero section features a macro-view visualization of high-hydration pasta dough being extruded through brass dies. The dough appears semi-translucent and viscous, moving with heavy, slow-motion physics. The background is stark white linen texture, contrasting with cold, brushed steel structural elements.

**Key Elements:**
1.  **Hero Animation:** A horizontal 'flow' of dough that reacts to mouse movement by changing viscosity (thickening or thinning). No particle effects; use vector-based fluid simulation or smooth CSS transforms.
2.  **Typography:** High-contrast serif for headings (e.g., 'Editorial New') to convey craft, paired with monospace for technical specs (e.g., 'IBM Plex Mono') to convey precision.
3.  **Color Palette:** Off-white (#F4F4F0), Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A), and Warm Beige (#C4B7A6) for the dough hue. Avoid saturated yellows.
4.  **Interaction:** Hovering over product cards causes the 'steel' borders to condense or frost over, simulating cold temperature.

**Constraints:**
- No rustic wood textures or farmhouse fonts.
- Maintain a sterile, laboratory-meets-kitchen aesthetic.
- Ensure fluid motion is 60fps and smooth (use GPU-accelerated transforms).
