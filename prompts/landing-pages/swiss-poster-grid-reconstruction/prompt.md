# Swiss Poster Grid Reconstruction

Design a landing page for 'Grid Theory', a studio dedicated to the International Typographic Style.

**Visual Style:**
- Background: Pure white (#ffffff) or off-white (#fafafa).
- Color Palette: Strictly Black (#000000) and Swiss Red (#ff0000). No other colors.
- Typography: Helvetica Neue or Inter Tight. Massive scale for headlines, small for metadata. All caps for headers.
- Layout: Modular grid system (e.g., 12 columns). Elements must align perfectly to grid lines. Use horizontal and vertical rules (thin black lines) to define space.

**Motion:**
- Elements enter the screen by sliding into their grid positions from off-canvas (left, right, top, bottom).
- Red accents (circles, triangles, squares) rotate slowly or scale on hover.
- Text lines reveal with a mask that moves strictly along the X-axis.

**Constraints:**
- No shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners (except for geometric circles).
- Whitespace is active design, not empty space.
