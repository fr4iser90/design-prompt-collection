01

Coat

Each datum is brushed onto the substrate as a latent image — chemically present, optically dark. Nothing is legible, nothing is trusted, until proven.

02

Expose

The verification beam scans the block. Where light lands, iron salts reduce and the record bleaches to white. The beam leaves no residue and cannot be aimed twice.

03

Fix

A confirmed block is stamped and sealed into the chain. Fixed prints resist further light — an immutable record, dated by the moment of its own exposure.
