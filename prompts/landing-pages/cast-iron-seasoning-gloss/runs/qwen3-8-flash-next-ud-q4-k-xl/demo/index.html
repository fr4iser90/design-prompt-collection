The Ritual

Seasoning is
not a coating.
It's chemistry.

Every Iron & Fire pan leaves the foundry with a polymerised base coat — but the true gloss is built by you, layer by layer, in your own kitchen. Heat, oil, and time fuse into a hard, slick, naturally non-stick surface.

FOUNDRY NOTE —
Our base coat is baked at 480°F in flaxseed oil for six hours. It arrives ready. What follows is yours.
01

Strip & Clean

Begin with bare, dry iron. A single drop of neutral oil, buffed until the surface looks almost dry — the sheen you can't quite see is the one that works.

STATE · BARE IRON · ROOM TEMP
02

Heat to Smoke

Bring the pan past its oil's smoke point. This is the moment the oil breaks down and begins to cross-link — the pan will whisper before it sings.

TARGET · 450–500°F · UNTIL SMOKING
03

Flip & Repeat

Let it cool, wipe, and go again. Five passes. Each layer darkens the surface and pushes the gloss from matte charcoal toward a true, oily black.

PASSES · ×5 · ~20 MIN EACH
04

Cook Through

The real seasoning is a life of searing. Fats, sugars, and Maillard crusts feed the surface. An old Iron & Fire pan holds light like a dark mirror.

GOAL · MIRROR BLACK · LIFETIME
The Craft

Built like it
outlives you.

Six decisions made in the foundry that turn a slab of iron into a piece you'll hand down.

SPEC / 01

Sand-Cast Body

Poured into green-sand moulds, not stamped. The grain is irregular, heavy, and unmistakably real under the hand.

SPEC / 02

Flaxseed Base Coat

Pre-seasoned at 480°F in cold-pressed flaxseed oil — a polymerised foundation that arrives slick and ready to cook.

SPEC / 03

Even Heat Retention

Thick walls hold temperature like memory. Sear a steak edge-to-edge and the pan barely notices the cold meat.

SPEC / 04

Stove, Oven, Fire

Induction to open flame, 300°F to 900°F. One pan crosses every heat source without a second thought.

SPEC / 05

Cast-Found Finish

Smoothed, not polished. A tactile, slightly pebbled surface that grips seasoning and releases food when it's hot enough.

SPEC / 06

Lifetime Guarantee

Re-seasoned free, forever. Cracks, warps, rust — bring it back to the foundry and we'll make it right.

The Flaxseed Standard
