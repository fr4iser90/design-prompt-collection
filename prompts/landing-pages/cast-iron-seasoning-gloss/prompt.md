# Cast Iron Seasoning Gloss

**Concept:** Landing page for 'Iron & Fire', premium cast iron cookware. The aesthetic is the 'perfect seasoning' — deep black, non-stick, with a subtle, oily sheen.

**Art Direction:**
- **Palette:** Matte black (#1A1A1A), charcoal grey (#333333), and warm white (#E0E0E0) for text. Accents in burnt orange (#CC5500) for heat references.
- **Texture:** Subtle cast iron grain noise. Specular highlights are key — they must look like oil/light on a curved surface.
- **Typography:** Display: 'Oswald' or 'Bebas Neue' (condensed, industrial, strong). Body: 'Source Code Pro' (technical, recipe-friendly).
- **Hero:** A large, dark, curved surface (representing a pan). A light source (cursor) moves a specular highlight across it.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Interaction:** Mouse movement controls a radial gradient 'light' on the pan surface, revealing the grain and gloss.
  2. **Entrance:** The 'heat' (orange glow) fades in from the edges of the pan, simulating pre-heating.
  3. **Scroll:** Sections are separated by 'cast iron' dividers (thick, dark lines with subtle pitting texture).

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/JS. Use CSS `background-image` with radial gradients for the light. Canvas optional for grain.
