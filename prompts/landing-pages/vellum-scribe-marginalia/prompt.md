# Vellum Scribe Marginalia

Design a landing page for 'Ink & Vellum', a premium calligraphy and manuscript restoration service. The aesthetic is 'warm-neutral institutional': clean, legible, and trustworthy, evoking a well-lit archive reading room.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** Pure, warm off-white (#FDFBF7) with a subtle, barely-there paper grain texture (not noisy, just a hint of fiber).
- **Typography:** A high-contrast, humanist serif for headings (e.g., 'Cormorant Garamond' or 'Playfair Display') and a clean, readable serif for body text. No sans-serifs.
- **The Gag:** The main content is typeset perfectly in two columns. However, elegant, handwritten marginalia (in a fine ink pen style, #4A3B32) appears in the margins, pointing to or commenting on specific phrases. These notes are static but feel 'added by hand'.
- **Layout:** Centered, max-width 1200px. Generous whitespace. No hero images, no cards. Just text, rules, and margins.

**Deliverable:** A single-page HTML/CSS implementation focusing on typography, spacing, and the visual weight of the marginalia.
