02 Atelier

Made in the heat

Every metre begins at a hand-loom beneath a courtyard of pale stone. We weave slowly, in the hours when the light is softest and the desert holds its breath.

Our fibres are sourced from a handful of growers we know by name. Dye is drawn from madder, indigo and the ochre of the ground itself — nothing synthetic touches the thread.
