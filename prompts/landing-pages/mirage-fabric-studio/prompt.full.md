# Mirage Fabric Studio — Extended

## Concept
Mirage Fabric Studio is a luxury textile house inspired by desert landscapes. The landing page must evoke fluidity, light, and high-end craftsmanship. The design language is 'arid-minimal' luxury: sparse, elegant, and atmospheric.

## Palette
- **Sun-Bleached White**: #F0EBE3 (Background, Light Text)
- **Sand Gold**: #C2B280 (Accents, Borders, Secondary Text)
- **Deep Shadow**: #2C2C2C (Primary Text, Dark Overlays)
- **Heat Haze**: Transparent with blur filter (Effect)

## Typography
- **Display**: Playfair Display Italic (Elegant, high-contrast)
- **Body**: Montserrat Light (Clean, modern)
- **Hierarchy**: Large, italic headings. Generous spacing.

## Layout
- **Hero**: Full-bleed looping video of silk fabric flowing in wind. Overlay: 'Mirage' in large Playfair Display, Deep Shadow with white text-shadow for contrast. CTA: 'View Collection' in Sand Gold, transparent border.
- **Navigation**: Fixed bottom, minimal icons in Deep Shadow. Hover: Sand Gold.
- **Sections**:
  1. **Collection**: Horizontal scroll gallery of fabric swatches. Swatches are long, thin strips (like fabric bolts). Hover: Strip expands, reveals fabric name in Playfair Display.
  2. **Atelier**: Split layout. Left: Text about craftsmanship in Montserrat. Right: Image of loom or hands weaving. Heat-haze effect applied to image edges.
  3. **Stockists**: Simple list of locations. Text in Deep Shadow. Hover: Link underline in Sand Gold.

## Motion
- **Entrance**: Hero video fades in. Text fades in with slight scale up (1.0 to 1.05).
- **Scroll**: Horizontal scroll for Collection gallery.
- **Interaction**: Hover on fabric strips triggers heat-haze distortion (CSS filter blur) on surrounding elements. Smooth color transitions to Sand Gold.
- **Fabric Flow**: Video background loops seamlessly.

## Constraints
- No rigid grids. Use fluid, organic layouts.
- No bright or synthetic colors.
- Video must be high-quality, seamless loop.
- Heat-haze effect must be subtle, not distracting.
- Responsive: Video covers full screen. Mobile layout stacks sections, horizontal scroll becomes vertical list.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Palette uses sun-bleached white, sand gold, deep shadow.
- Typography is Playfair Display (elegant serif).
- Hero is video background of flowing fabric.
- Horizontal scroll used for collection.
- Heat-haze effect on hover.
