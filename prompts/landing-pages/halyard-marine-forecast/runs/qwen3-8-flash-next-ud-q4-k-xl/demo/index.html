Case log — winter crossing, Rotterdam → Halifax

Route 04 · 11–18 November · 2,940 NM · 68,000 dwt bulk carrier.

The great-circle track ran straight into a 992-hectopascal low building over the Rockall Trough. Halyard's routing engine held the vessel 14 nautical miles south through the first two waypoints, trading distance against a two-day window of 45-knot gusts.

Mid-ocean, the engine re-solved the track twice against falling barometric readings from the vessel's own sensors — a feedback loop between forecast and observed sea state that no static chart offers. The crossing finished with a net fuel saving and a materially calmer ride for cargo and crew.
