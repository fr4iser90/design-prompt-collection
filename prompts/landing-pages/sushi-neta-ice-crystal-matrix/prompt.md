# Sushi Neta Ice Crystal Matrix

Design a landing page for 'Tsuru Cold Chain', a luxury bluefin tuna and sashimi supplier.

**Visual Concept:**
The hero visual is a high-resolution macro of a fish slice (Toro) suspended in a clear, crystalline structure. The 'ice' is not messy slush but geometric, faceted crystals that refract light. The background is deep midnight blue (#0F172A), simulating the deep ocean.

**Key Elements:**
1.  **Hero Interaction:** The 'ice matrix' cracks slightly on hover, revealing the vibrant red/pink of the fish beneath. This is not violent; it is a precise, laser-like reveal.
2.  **Temperature Overlay:** A subtle, animated digital overlay shows the internal temperature of the fish (-20°C) in thin, monospace cyan text (#38BDF8).
3.  **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif (e.g., 'Inter' or 'Helvetica Now') but with very high kerning for a spaced-out, premium feel. No decorative serifs.
4.  **Color Palette:** Deep Ocean Blue (#0F172A), Ice Blue (#38BDF8), and Fish Pink/Red (natural photo colors).

**Constraints:**
- No bamboo, no wood, no traditional Japanese patterns (seigaiha, etc.) unless abstracted to extreme minimalism.
- Focus on *cold* and *clarity*, not 'warm hospitality'.
- Images must be high-fidelity macro photography of texture.
