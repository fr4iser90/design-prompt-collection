# Ceramic Insulator Curve

Create a landing page for 'Porcelain & Flux', a high-voltage ceramic insulator manufacturer. The design language is 'Soft-Machine': warm, tactile, and precise.

**Visuals:**
- Background: Warm off-white (`#FDFBF7`) with a subtle paper or stone texture.
- Hero: An animated line graph representing the hysteresis loop of ceramic insulation. The line is drawn in a warm terracotta or deep brown (`#8A8177`).
- Elements: Use shapes inspired by ceramic insulators (ribbed discs, bells). These shapes can be used as containers for content.
- Typography: 'Editorial New' or 'Canela' for headlines (serif, elegant). 'Söhne' or 'Aktiv Grotesk' for body.
- Motion: The hysteresis loop draws itself slowly on load. Hovering over the loop highlights the 'resistance' and 'capacity' zones with subtle background color shifts.

**Constraints:**
- No cold blues or tech-neons.
- No heavy shadows. Use soft, diffuse shadows if necessary.
- Focus on the material qualities: porcelain, ceramic, clay.
- No Inter, Roboto, Arial.
