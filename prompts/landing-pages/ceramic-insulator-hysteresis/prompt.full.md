# Ceramic Insulator Curve — Extended Brief

## Concept
'Porcelain & Flux' combines traditional material science (ceramics) with modern electrical engineering. The website should feel warm, grounded, and trustworthy. The core visual is the hysteresis loop, a key concept in material science, presented as an elegant, artistic data visualization.

## Palette
- **Background:** `#FDFBF7` (Warm Off-White)
- **Primary Accent:** `#8A8177` (Warm Taupe/Stone)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#E8E2D9` (Light Sand)
- **Text:** `#5C534A` (Dark Warm Grey)
- **Highlight:** `#D4C9BA` (Beige)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** 'Editorial New' or 'Canela'. Serif, high contrast, elegant.
- **Body:** 'Söhne' or 'Aktiv Grotesk'. Clean sans-serif, readable.
- **Data/Labels:** 'IBM Plex Mono'. Small, monospaced.

## Layout
- **Hero:** Split screen. Left: Headline and brief description. Right: Large, interactive SVG hysteresis loop.
- **Product Section:** Cards shaped like ceramic insulator discs (circular or oval). Inside, technical specs and images.
- **Footer:** Simple, centered, with contact info and a subtle pattern.

## Motion Design
- **Entrance:** The hysteresis loop path animates its `stroke-dashoffset` to draw itself in a continuous, smooth motion. The text fades in.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over the loop triggers a tooltip showing specific data points (e.g., 'Breakdown Voltage', 'Dielectric Constant'). The hovered section of the loop thickens or glows subtly.
- **Ambient:** A very slow, subtle pulse of the background color (barely perceptible) to suggest energy.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] No Inter, Roboto, Arial.
- [ ] No purple/green tech colors.
- [ ] Ensure the serif font is legible at small sizes (or use sans-serif for small text).
- [ ] Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The hysteresis loop is accurate and visually appealing.
- The warm palette feels cohesive and distinct from typical industrial sites.
- The site conveys a sense of material quality and engineering precision.
