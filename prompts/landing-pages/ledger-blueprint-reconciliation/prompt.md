# Ledger Blueprint Reconciliation

Create a landing page for "VeriLedger," a financial audit and reconciliation tool.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** Pure white (#ffffff) overlaid with a faint, light-blue blueprint grid pattern.
- **Typography:** Sans-serif but highly structured. Use a mono-spaced font (e.g., JetBrains Mono) for numbers and data, and a clean sans (e.g., Work Sans) for headings.
- **Key Element:** A hero section showing a "reconciliation" animation where two columns of numbers align perfectly with a satisfying click sound visual (a green checkmark appearing).
- **Color Palette:** White, Deep Teal (#005f73), Bright Teal (#0a9396), and a hint of Gold (#e9d8a6) for "verified" states.
- **Layout:** Strong vertical alignment. Everything must feel aligned to the grid. Use lines to connect data points visually.

**Deliverable:** A hero section and one feature section demonstrating the "alignment" metaphor.
