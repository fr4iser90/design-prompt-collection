# Butcher Block Grain Map

**Concept:** An interactive landing page for a professional knife brand. The background is a hyper-realistic wood grain texture (end-grain butcher block). Knives are not just images; they are interactive elements that 'slice' the grain to reveal information.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Texture:** High-res SVG or CSS pattern of end-grain wood. The grain should run vertically.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over a knife image creates a 'cut' effect—a thin, dark line (blade) that slices through the grain. The grain parts slightly around the blade.
- **Typography:** Industrial sans-serif (e.g., *Roboto Condensed* or *Oswald*) in black. Stamped aesthetic.
- **Layout:** Horizontal scroll or a map-like layout where different 'zones' (Chef, Paring, Boning) are marked by the grain's natural lines.
- **Motion:** Sharp, instant transitions. No floaty animations. The cut should feel mechanical and precise.

**Deliverable:** A single-page HTML/CSS/JS implementation featuring a wood-grain background with hover-triggered 'slice' animations on product cards.
