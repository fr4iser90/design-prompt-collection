# Spice Mill Grind Torque

Create a landing page for 'Torque & Spice'.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Background:** Matte Black (#111111) with subtle brushed metal texture.
- **Hero Element:** A central, stylized grinder icon. As the user scrolls, the grinder rotates (CSS transform rotate). Ground spice particles (dots) fall from the bottom of the grinder.
- **Interaction:** The falling particles accumulate at the bottom to form the headline text ('Freshness in Every Grind'). The amount of particles increases with scroll depth.
- **Typography:** Bold, industrial sans-serif. Headline in Light Sand (#d4a373). Body in Light Grey (#a3a3a3).
- **Layout:** Central focus. Grinder at top, text accumulation at bottom.
- **Motion:** Scroll-driven rotation. Particles fall with gravity-like easing.

**Constraints:**
- No colorful spice jars.
- No 'earthy' green backgrounds.
- Particles must look like ground spice, not sand.
