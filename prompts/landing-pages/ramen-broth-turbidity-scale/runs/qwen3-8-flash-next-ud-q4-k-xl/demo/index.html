The philosophy

Clarity is a
starting point, not
a destination.

A bowl of ramen is a suspension of time. Collagen, fat and mineral slowly leave the bone and enter the water until the light can no longer pass through it. We don't hide this process — we chart it, ladle by ladle, so you can taste exactly how long you've waited.

01Emulsion, not seasoningDepth comes from suspended fat, not salt. We chase the cloud.
02Read the brothEvery cup carries its own turbidity index, printed on the sleeve.
03No shortcutsNothing is thickened. If it isn't cloudy from the bone, it isn't ours.
The Turbidity Scale

Five states of
the same water.

01Clear
0–20 NTU

Filtered water. The blank canvas — bright, weightless, ready to receive.

02Light
20–80 NTU

First draw. A whisper of gelatin; the broth begins to hold its own shape.

03Milky
80–160 NTU

Fat and collagen emulsify. Light scatters. The spoon begins to feel coated.

04Opaque
160–260 NTU

Rich and heavy. The bowl is now a body, not a liquid — you can taste the hours.

05Saturated
260–320 NTU

Full tonkotsu. Creamy, clinging, near-solid at the edges. The end of the line.
