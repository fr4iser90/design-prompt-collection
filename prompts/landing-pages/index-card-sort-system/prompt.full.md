# Index Card Sort System — Extended

## Concept
"SortWell" is a digital tool that brings the tactile, intuitive nature of physical index cards to research management. The design should feel like a desk covered in organized notes. The use of "hand-drawn" underlines and ink colors adds a human touch, suggesting that this tool supports organic, creative thinking rather than rigid, automated processes. It’s about control and clarity, not just storage.

## Palette
- **Paper White:** #f5f5f0 (Background)
- **Ink Black:** #333333 (Primary Text)
- **Red Pen:** #d32f2f (Highlights, Alerts)
- **Blue Pen:** #1976d2 (Links, Tags)
- **Graphite:** #666666 (Secondary Text)

## Typography
- **Headings:** "Helvetica Now" or "Arial" (used carefully to avoid generic feel). Clean, bold.
- **Body:** "Georgia" or "Times New Roman". Serif for readability and academic feel.
- **Handwriting:** Use a web font like "Caveat" or "Shadows Into Light" for underlines and margin notes.

## Layout
- **Hero:** A stack of 3-5 index cards in the center. The top card has the headline. Hovering shuffles the top card to reveal the next.
- **Features:** Each feature is a card. Cards are arranged in a loose grid, not rigid. Some cards are slightly rotated (2-3 degrees) to look natural.
- **Background:** The paper texture should be subtle, not overpowering.

## Motion
- **Stack Hover:** On hovering the hero stack, the top card slides up and to the right, revealing the card below.
- **Sort Animation:** In a feature section, show cards moving from a messy pile to aligned rows.
- **Underline:** When hovering a link, the hand-drawn underline animates in (stroke-dashoffset).

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] No dark mode.
- [ ] No sharp, digital borders. Cards should have slight rounded corners and soft shadows.
- [ ] Hand-drawn elements must look intentional, not messy.
- [ ] The "paper" texture must be high-resolution to avoid pixelation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The page feels warm, human, and organized.
- The index card metaphor is consistent throughout.
- The interaction feels tactile and satisfying.
