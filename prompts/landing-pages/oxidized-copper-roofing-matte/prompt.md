# Oxidized Copper Roofing

**Concept:** A landing page for 'Verdigris Architects', a firm specializing in oxidized copper facades. The design embodies the slow, beautiful decay and stabilization of copper into verdigris (green patina). Object honesty over UI chrome.

**Art Direction:**
- **Palette:** Deep charcoal (#2F4F4F), oxidized bronze/brown (#6A5C52), and pale verdigris green (#8FA898) accents. Background is off-white matte paper (#EAE6D9) or dark slate.
- **Texture:** Subtle grain overlay mimicking oxidized metal surface. No glossy UI elements. Buttons should feel like pressed metal or matte stone.
- **Typography:** Display: 'Cormorant Garamond' (serif) for headings, evoking classical architecture. Body: 'IBM Plex Sans' (technical, readable).
- **Layout:** Asymmetric grid with wide gutters. Hero section features a large, abstract geometric shape representing a roofline, filled with a gradient of bronze to green.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Entrance:** Fade-in with a subtle 'oxidation' spread effect (green tint slowly blooms across the hero shape).
  2. **Ambient:** Slow, nearly imperceptible shift in the metallic gradient of the background elements, simulating time passing.
  3. **Interaction:** Hovering over project thumbnails reveals a 'weathered' state (slightly darker, more textured).

**Deliverable:** Single-page HTML/CSS/JS. Use SVG for hero geometry. Ensure accessibility (contrast ratios > 4.5:1).
