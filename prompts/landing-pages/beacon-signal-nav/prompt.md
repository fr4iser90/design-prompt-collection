# Beacon Signal Nav

Design a landing page for 'Beacon,' a maritime navigation AI. The aesthetic is 'night-watch bridge': deep ocean blues, high-visibility safety green, and radar-inspired interfaces.

**Visual Rules:**
- Background: Deep Navy/Black (#05141f).
- Accent: Safety Green (#00ff9d) for active states, data lines, and CTAs. Use sparingly for maximum impact.
- Typography: 'Inter' or 'Roboto Mono' for data; 'Montserrat' for headings. Clean, geometric, legible in low light.
- Imagery: Dark, moody ocean photography with light reflections. Use CSS masks to blend images into the dark background.
- Elements: Circular radar elements, concentric circles, thin 1px lines.

**Motion & Interaction:**
- **Ambient:** A slow, rotating 'radar sweep' gradient overlay in the hero section.
- **Hover:** Navigation items light up like illuminated buttons; hover triggers a faint 'ping' ripple effect.
- **Scroll:** Parallax layers for ocean waves. Data points on the map pulse gently.

**Deliverable:** Hero with a live-looking radar element, plus a 'Capabilities' section with line-drawn icons.
