# Swim Lane Caustics Flow

**Concept:** Landing page for 'AquaFlow', a competitive swimming coaching platform. The entire interface feels submerged, with light refraction patterns dancing over the content.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Deep Ocean Blue (#0057B7), Bright Cyan (#00A8E8), Foam White (#E0F7FA), Midnight (#001F3F).
- **Typography:** Humanist sans-serif with soft terminals (e.g., 'Lato' or 'Open Sans' but customized). Rounded edges.
- **Layout:** Floating cards with high transparency. No hard borders; use shadows to imply depth underwater.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Ambient:** A WebGL or CSS-based caustic light effect animates across the background (white moving light patterns on blue).
  2. **Float:** Content elements gently bob up and down (translateY) at different speeds/phases.
  3. **Scroll:** Parallax layers move at different speeds to simulate water resistance.
