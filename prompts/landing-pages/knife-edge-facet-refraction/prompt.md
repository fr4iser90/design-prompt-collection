# Knife Edge Facet Refraction

Design a landing page for 'Apex Steel', a high-end chef's knife manufacturer.

**Visual Concept:**
The hero is a macro close-up of the knife's cutting edge (the 'kiss'). The edge is so sharp it is nearly invisible, but it catches a sliver of light. The background is pure black (#000000). The knife itself is rendered in shades of silver/grey (#8A8A8A to #E0E0E0).

**Key Elements:**
1.  **Hero Interaction:** As the user scrolls or moves the mouse, the angle of the knife changes, causing the specular highlight on the edge to slide and flare. This mimics the act of testing the edge.
2.  **Typography:** Ultra-thin, elegant sans-serif (e.g., 'Didot' or 'Bodoni' for headings, 'Lato Light' for body). The text should feel light and sharp.
3.  **Color Palette:** Black (#000000), Silver (#C0C0C0), White (#FFFFFF). No wood, no gold, no color.
4.  **Product Cards:** Minimalist. Just the knife silhouette against black, with a single thin white line indicating the blade length.

**Constraints:**
- No 'rustic' or 'vintage' elements.
- The focus is on *sharpness* and *light*, not material history.
- Use of gradients must be subtle and metallic, not colorful.
