# Gridlock Transit Alert

Design a landing page for a city transit authority's real-time disruption hub. Use a utility aesthetic: heavy hazard stripes (diagonal yellow/black) as structural dividers, not decoration. Typography must be strictly monospaced for data integrity (e.g., Space Mono, IBM Plex Mono) paired with a bold, condensed sans-serif for headlines (e.g., Oswald, Archivo Narrow).

**Visual Rules:**
- Background: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a) to maximize contrast for signal colors.
- Accents: Safety Yellow (#ffcc00) for 'Caution/Update', Alert Red (#ff3333) for 'Critical/Stop'.
- Layout: Modular grid resembling a station departure board. No rounded corners; use sharp 2px borders or hard edges.
- Imagery: Abstract schematics of tracks/lines, high-contrast line art only.

**Motion & Interaction:**
- Subtle 'pulse' on active alert rows.
- Hover states on route cards trigger a diagonal stripe fill animation.
- Scroll reveals: Sections snap into place like a mechanical flip-board.

**Deliverable:** A responsive landing page hero + disruption list section.
