# PulseLane Urban Mobility — Extended

Concept: PulseLane is a bike courier routing product for dense cities. The landing page should feel kinetic, navigational, and high-contrast, like a transit map rendered at night. Lead with the brand and a route line that crosses the screen, then explain speed, coverage, and pricing through motion and sparse copy.

Palette: #0b1020 for the asphalt-blue base, #d8ff3f for fluorescent lime route accents, and #37c8ff for electric blue secondary highlights and interactive states.

Type pairing: Canela for display headlines, Public Sans for body and UI, and IBM Plex Mono for route labels, distances, and city codes.

Layout desktop: use a wide hero with a full-bleed animated path, a brand block anchored top left, a metrics rail at bottom, then sections for network, pricing, and city coverage. Keep generous negative space between map-like elements.

Layout mobile: collapse to a single column with a simplified route line, stacked metrics, sticky CTA, and a clean pricing list.

Motion brief: entrance: dashed path draws across the hero and headline letters settle; ambient: route nodes pulse softly; interaction: cursor or touch reveals a pin label with distance and time.

Constraints: no purple glow, no glassmorphism, no hero cards, no more than 3 motion types, and no decorative confetti or generic city stock imagery.

Acceptance criteria: the brand reads first, the route metaphor is clear, palette feels urban and electric, motion supports navigation, and the page is responsive and self-contained.

Variants: A uses a horizontal route path. B uses a vertical transit line with the same palette and type.
