# Gym Rack Iron Structure

**Concept:** Landing page for 'IronGrid', a commercial gym equipment manufacturer. The design uses the visual language of a squat rack: exposed steel beams, safety pins, and weight plates.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Gunmetal Grey (#4A4A4A), Steel Light (#999999), Matte Black (#1A1A1A), Chrome Highlight (#C0C0C0).
- **Typography:** Industrial stencil or heavy block sans (e.g., 'Bebas Neue' or 'Teko'). Uppercase only.
- **Layout:** A strict 8-column grid that looks like exposed steel beams. Vertical lines are thick (4px) and dark.
- **Motion:**
  1. **Snap:** Elements don't fade in; they 'drop' into place with a slight bounce, simulating weight.
  2. **Load:** Hovering a product card increases the 'shadow' depth, as if the weight is increasing.
  3. **Click:** Safety pin animation locks the menu open.
