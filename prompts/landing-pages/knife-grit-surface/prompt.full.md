# Knife & Grit Surface — Extended Brief

## Concept
This landing page is about sharpness. Every element should feel cut, precise, and intentional. It avoids clutter, focusing on the interplay between the blade (steel) and the stone (grit). The aesthetic is industrial and tactical, appealing to chefs, hunters, and collectors.

## Palette & Materials
- **Background:** Near Black (`#18181B`) to emphasize the reflective qualities of steel.
- **Text:** Bright White (`#E4E4E7`) for maximum contrast.
- **Accents:** Metallic Grays (`#A1A1AA`, `#3F3F46`) to represent stone and steel tones.
- **Textures:** Brushed steel, rough stone grain, water droplets on metal.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** `Oswald` or `Bebas Neue` (all caps, tight tracking). The letters should feel like they are carved into the page.
- **Body:** `Roboto` or `Open Sans` for clear, concise instructions.
- **Data:** `Courier New` or `Space Mono` for grit numbers (e.g., 1000, 3000, 6000).

## Layout & Structure
1. **Hero:** A split screen. Left: Large, bold text "SHARPEN YOUR EDGE." Right: A macro video loop of a knife being drawn across a whetstone, with sparks or water mist. 
2. **Anatomy Section:** A diagram of a knife blade, labeled with parts (spine, edge, bolster). Use thin white lines and dots for annotations. 
3. **Grit Guide:** A horizontal scale showing different grit levels. As the user scrolls or interacts, the background texture changes from rough stone to polished steel, reflecting the grit level.
4. **Product Grid:** Clean, isolated product shots of stones and honing tools. Each card has a subtle steel gradient border.

## Motion Design
- **Entrance:** Text slides in from the left with a sharp, fast easing function (e.g., `cubic-bezier(0.1, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`).
- **Ambient:** A slow, sweeping light reflection (glint) moves across the hero image periodically.
- **Interaction:** Hovering over product images causes a slight tilt (3D transform) to show depth. 
- **Scroll:** Parallax on the background textures (stone vs. steel) to create a sense of layering.

## Constraints
- No rounded corners; use sharp 90-degree angles for UI elements to reinforce the "knife" theme.
- Keep the color palette strictly monochrome with subtle warm highlights on the steel.
- Avoid any food imagery; focus on the tool and the craft.
