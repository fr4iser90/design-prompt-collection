03 / THE TECHNIQUE

Four Moves
To A Mirror

Precision is a habit. Keep the angle constant, let the stone do the work, and finish on the finest grit.

01

Soak

Submerge the water stone until bubbles stop rising. A saturated surface carries the slurry that cuts.

10–15 min
02

Coarse Grind

Set a fixed 15° angle and draw across the 1000 grit. Work each side until a burr forms along the full edge.

2–3 min / side
03

Refine

Step to 3000 grit, then 6000. Lighten pressure as grit rises — you're polishing, not removing.

1 min / grit
04

Strop & Test

Remove the burr on leather, then test against paper. A true edge falls through with zero resistance.

30 sec
