# Knife & Grit Surface

Design a landing page for 'Edge & Stone', a brand for premium knife sharpening stones and tools.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Atmosphere:** Cold, sharp, precise. High-contrast monochrome with subtle warm steel highlights.
- **Palette:** Deep Black (`#18181B`) background, Sharp White (`#E4E4E7`) text, and various grays for steel/stone textures.
- **Typography:** Bold, condensed sans-serif (like `Oswald` or `Bebas Neue`) for impact, paired with a readable sans for body text.
- **Hero:** Extreme close-up of a knife blade catching light, cutting through a visual barrier. Sharp, angular lines.
- **Sections:** 'The Edge' (anatomy of a knife), 'The Stone' (grit levels visualized), 'The Technique' (step-by-step sharpening).
- **Motion:** Sharp, quick transitions. Glints of light sweeping across steel surfaces.
