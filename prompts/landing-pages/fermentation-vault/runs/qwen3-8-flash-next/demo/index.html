Chapter One

The Process

Six Stages · Thirty Days · One Transformation
01/ 06
Inoculation

Fermentation is not a recipe. It is a conversation between salt, sugar, time, and the microbes you invite in. Follow the stages as they unfold — the deeper you go, the darker the vault.

Day 00Inoculation

Inoculation

Salt, water, and the culture meet. Oxygen is sealed out. The anaerobic work begins before you can see a single change.

Day 02Bloom

Bloom

The first bubbles rise — carbon dioxide from hungry bacteria. The amber glow inside the glass deepens as the culture awakens.

Day 07Colony

Colony

Acids lower the pH and crowd out the unwanted. The surface settles. What was raw is becoming preserved, alive, and stable.

Day 14Settling

Settling

Flavour compounds accumulate. Sediment drifts to the base while the liquid clarifies into a warm, translucent amber.

Day 21Deep Work

Deep Work

The slowest, most important stage. Complexity builds in the dark, at a constant temperature, with nothing disturbed.

Day 30Harvest

Harvest

Seal, cellar, and taste. What began as vegetables and salt is now something no single ingredient could have been alone.
