# Fermentation Vault — Extended Brief

## Concept
Fermentation is a slow, transformative process involving invisible life. This landing page should feel like a vault or a sanctuary for these living cultures. It avoids the "hippie" or "rustic" tropes of fermented food, instead presenting it as a sophisticated, almost alchemical practice.

## Palette & Materials
- **Background:** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#111111`) to make the internal glows pop.
- **Accent:** Amber/Gold (`#F59E0B`) for highlights, buttons, and the "glow" effect inside jars.
- **Support:** Warm Gray (`#3F3F46`) for secondary text and borders.
- **Textures:** Glass refractions, matte ceramic, wet organic textures (subtle), and paper-like labels.

## Typography
- **Display:** `Cormorant Garamond` or `Playfair Display` for headings. Evokes tradition and history.
- **Body/UI:** `Lato` or `Source Sans Pro` for readability. Clean and unobtrusive.
- **Labels:** Small-caps serif for section labels (e.g., "CULTURE NO. 4").

## Layout & Structure
1. **Hero:** A cinematic, dark hero section. Several large glass crocks are arranged asymmetrically. Each emits a soft, pulsing amber light from within. The headline is centered, white, and elegant: "Time is the Ingredient."
2. **The Process:** A horizontal scroll section showing the stages of fermentation. Images are macro shots of bubbles, mold, or brine, labeled with days/weeks. The background darkens as you scroll deeper into the "process."
3. **The Vessels:** A grid of ceramic crocks and glass jars. Hovering over a product makes it glow faintly from the inside. Include technical specs: volume, material, seal type.
4. **Culture Library:** A card-based layout introducing different fermentation cultures (kimchi, kombucha, sourdough). Each card has a unique texture preview and a "starter kit" CTA.

## Motion Design
- **Ambient:** The light inside the jars in the hero should pulse very slowly (4-6 seconds per cycle), mimicking the slow metabolism of bacteria.
- **Bubbles:** Subtle CSS or JS-driven bubbles rising in the hero background.
- **Scroll:** Elements fade in and scale up slightly (0.95 to 1.0) as they enter the viewport. 
- **Interaction:** Hovering over "The Process" stages expands the image slightly and brightens the amber accent.

## Constraints
- Avoid green/earthy tones unless strictly necessary for specific cultures.
- Keep the interface minimal; let the images and light do the work.
- Ensure the glow effects do not look like cheap CSS box-shadows; use radial gradients and blur for realism.
