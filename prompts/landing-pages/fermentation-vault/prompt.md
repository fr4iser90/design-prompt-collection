# Fermentation Vault

Create a landing page for 'Microbe & Time', a brand selling high-end fermentation kits and crocks.

**Visual Direction:**
- **Atmosphere:** Mysterious, ancient, yet scientific. Like a library of living cultures.
- **Palette:** Near-black background (`#111111`) with warm, glowing amber accents (`#F59E0B`) representing the glow of active fermentation inside glass.
- **Typography:** A classic serif (like `Cormorant Garamond`) for the brand story, paired with a technical sans-serif for product specs.
- **Hero:** A dark scene with multiple glass jars, each lit from within by a soft amber glow. Slow, bubbling animation.
- **Sections:** 'The Cultures' (interactive timeline of fermentation stages), 'The Vessels' (ceramic textures), 'The Kitchen' (minimalist, dark kitchen setup).
- **Motion:** Slow, organic movements. Bubbles rising, light shifting subtly. No fast transitions.
