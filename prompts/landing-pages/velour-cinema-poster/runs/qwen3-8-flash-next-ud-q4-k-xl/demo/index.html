01 The Story

A confession told in velvet and smoke.

Detective Lena Vane returns to the Amber Room — a speakeasy that has no address and no owner — chasing a case the department buried thirty years ago. The woman who vanished left behind a single coat, still warm, and a name no one dares to say twice.

As the city's lights gutter and die one by one, Lena discovers that the truth is not a fact to be found but a feeling to be worn. In Velour, nothing is what it appears under the low gold light — and everyone is performing for an audience that never blinks.
