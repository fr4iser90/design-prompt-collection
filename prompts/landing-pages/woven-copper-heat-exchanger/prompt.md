# WeaveWorks: Copper Heat Exchanger Hero

Design a landing page for 'WeaveWorks', industrial heat exchangers.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Hero Element:** A large, abstract SVG of woven copper tubes (like a basket weave but made of pipes).
- **Material:** Copper. Use gradients to simulate metallic sheen (#b87333). 
- **Motion:** 
  1. **Pulse:** A warm orange glow (#ff5500) travels through the pipes in a rhythmic loop, simulating heat transfer.
  2. **Tighten:** On scroll, the weave 'tightens' (scale down slightly, opacity increases), symbolizing efficiency.
- **Layout:** Hero fills the screen. Text is overlayed with a mix-blend-mode (difference or exclusion) to stand out against the complex background.
- **Typography:** Modern, geometric sans-serif. White text.
- **Background:** Very dark, almost black (#1a1a1a) to let the copper glow.

**Deliverable:** HTML/CSS/SVG with animation logic for the heat pulse.
