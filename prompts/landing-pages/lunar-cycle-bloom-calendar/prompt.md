# Lunar Cycle Bloom Calendar

Create a dark-mode landing page for 'Selene & Stem', a curator of rare night-blooming seeds.

**Visual Core:**
- **Background:** Deep midnight blue (#0f172a) with subtle star-field noise (low opacity, static).
- **Hero Element:** A large, circular 'Lunar Dial' in the center. It acts as a clock and a calendar.
- **Interaction:** As the user scrolls or hovers, the moon phase within the dial shifts. When the 'full moon' phase aligns, specific night-blooming botanical illustrations (Moonflower, Night Jasmine) bloom open in real-time SVG animation.
- **Typography:** High-contrast serif for headings (elegant, sharp), monospace for data labels (lunar age, illumination %).

**Constraints:**
- No bright greens; use muted teals, pale lavenders, and silver/white for the plants.
- The bloom animation must be slow and viscous, not snappy.
- Mobile: The dial shrinks; the bloom sequence is triggered by scroll position rather than hover.

**Deliverable:**
- Responsive HTML/CSS/JS with SVG animations for the dial and flowers.
