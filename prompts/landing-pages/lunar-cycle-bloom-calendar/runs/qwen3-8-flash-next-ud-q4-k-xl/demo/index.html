The Ritual

Three acts, performed in the dark.

01

Sow by phase

Each packet names its lunar window. Turn the soil only when the moon agrees — the seed reads the light before it reads the season.

Day 09 – Day 16
02

Keep the vigil

From dusk, sit in stillness. Do not shine a torch. The bloom is shy; it opens to the absence of the sun, not the presence of a lamp.

20:00 – 23:00
03

Harvest the perfume

The scent peaks for a single hour before the petals fold. Gather it then, or gather nothing at all. This is the whole of the bargain.
