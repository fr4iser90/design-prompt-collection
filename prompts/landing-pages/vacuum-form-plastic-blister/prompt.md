# Vacuum-Form Blister

Design a landing page for 'Form & Void', a custom vacuum-forming factory. The core visual metaphor is the vacuum-formed plastic blister: clear, deep, and precise.

**Visuals:**
- Background: Pure white with subtle, large-scale geometric shadows to suggest depth.
- Hero: An interactive 3D-like SVG of a vacuum-formed tray. It contains 'products' (abstract shapes) that snap into the cavities.
- Lighting: Soft, diffuse north-light. Highlights should be subtle white gradients on the edges of the plastic.
- Typography: 'Helvetica Now' or 'Neue Montreal'. Bold headlines, light body.
- Motion: Parallax effect on the plastic layers. When scrolling, the 'lid' of the blister lifts slightly to reveal the contents. Hovering over a cavity makes it glow softly.

**Constraints:**
- Avoid plastic-looking clichés (cheap glossy reflections).
- Use realistic refraction simulation if possible, or clean vector shapes with gradient edges.
- Colors: White, light grey, medium grey, dark grey text.
- No dark mode. The aesthetic is sterile, clean, and bright.
