01 — Method

Four moves from sheet to shelf.

Every blister begins as a flat thermoplastic sheet and ends as a form that holds your product like a cast.

STEP 01

Mold

We CNC a negative tool from your CAD — aluminum or composite, tuned to your draft angles.

STEP 02

Heat

The sheet warms in a radiant oven until it drapes, pliable and evenly translucent.

STEP 03

Vacuum

A vacuum pulls the hot sheet down onto the tool, capturing every wall, rib, and corner.

STEP 04

Trim

A die cuts the formed part clean, deburring edges for a flawless, retail-ready finish.
