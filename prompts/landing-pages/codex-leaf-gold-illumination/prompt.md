# Codex Leaf Gold Illumination

Create a landing page for "Aurelia Archives," a high-end digital repository for historical manuscripts.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Background:** A subtle, high-resolution texture of aged vellum or heavy cream paper (#fdfbf7).
- **Typography:** Use a classic, high-contrast serif for headings (e.g., Cormorant Garamond) with simulated gold-leaf effects on initial drop caps. Body text in a clean, readable serif (e.g., EB Garamond) in dark sepia (#2c241b).
- **Layout:** Single-column, centered, generous whitespace. No cards, no shadows. Elements are separated by thin, ornate horizontal rules.
- **Motion:** Static or extremely subtle. No scrolling parallax. Interaction is limited to a gentle color shift on links from sepia to gold.
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet, reverent, academic. Trust through permanence, not speed.

**Deliverable:** A single-page HTML/CSS implementation focusing on typography hierarchy and texture.
