# Codex Leaf Gold Illumination — Extended

## Concept
"Aurelia Archives" is a platform for preserving and displaying rare historical manuscripts. The design language rejects modern UI trends (cards, shadows, vibrant colors) in favor of the aesthetic of the illuminated manuscript itself. The interface should feel like turning a page in a library, where the "tech" is invisible, serving only to present the content with dignity and clarity. The gold accents represent value, permanence, and the divine nature of recorded knowledge.

## Palette
- **Vellum White:** #fdfbf7 (Background)
- **Iron Gall Ink:** #2c241b (Primary Text)
- **Burnished Gold:** #d4af37 (Accents, Drop Caps)
- **Aged Leather:** #8b7355 (Secondary Text, Borders)

## Typography
- **Display:** "Cormorant Garamond" or "Playfair Display". High contrast, elegant, traditional.
- **Body:** "EB Garamond" or "Gentium Book Plus". Readable, serif, scholarly.
- **Effect:** Initial letters of major sections should be styled as "drop caps" with a CSS gradient or image mask simulating gold leaf reflection.

## Layout
- **Desktop:** Centered container, max-width 800px. Vertical rhythm is key. Large margins between sections.
- **Mobile:** Full width with significant padding. Drop caps scale down but remain visible.
- **Navigation:** Minimal. A simple top bar with text links, separated by a thin hairline rule.

## Motion
- **Entrance:** Fade-in of the entire page, as if light is slowly illuminating a page.
- **Interaction:** Links underline with a thin gold line. No hover scaling or lifting. The interaction should feel like touching paper, not a digital button.

## Constraints Checklist
- [ ] No sans-serif fonts.
- [ ] No dark mode.
- [ ] No drop shadows or box shadows.
- [ ] No animated backgrounds or particles.
- [ ] Gold effect must be subtle, not tacky or flashy.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The page feels calm and authoritative.
- Typography is crisp and legible at all sizes.
- The gold accents add value without overwhelming the text.
- The layout respects the vertical rhythm of a manuscript.
