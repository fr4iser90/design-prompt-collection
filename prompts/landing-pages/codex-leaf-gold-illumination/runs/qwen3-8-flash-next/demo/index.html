The KeepingOn the dignity of the page

We hold that a manuscript is not merely information to be retrieved, but an object to be revered. Each volume in the Aurelia collection is captured at archival resolution, its vellum grain, its iron-gall ink, and the burnished gleam of its illumination rendered with the fidelity the original deserves. Nothing is flattened for speed. The interface recedes so the page may speak.

Our method is patient by design. Scans are calibrated against the physical leaf; colour is managed under controlled light; provenance is recorded alongside the image. What you read here is the manuscript as it endures — quiet, permanent, and true.

ProvenanceA chain of custody, unbroken

Every object in the archive carries its own record of ownership, conservation, and capture. We publish the full lineage of each manuscript — from the scriptorium that first set quill to parchment, through the libraries and collectors who sheltered it, to the bench where our imaging was performed. Trust, for us, is not a promise; it is a document.

Should a leaf ever be returned to its source community, the digital record follows, credited and attributed in perpetuity.
